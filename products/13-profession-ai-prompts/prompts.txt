[T-01] Lesson plan generator:
"Create a detailed 45-minute lesson plan for [subject] on the topic of [topic] for [grade level] students. Include: learning objectives, warm-up activity (5 min), main instruction (20 min), guided practice (10 min), independent activity (5 min), wrap-up/exit ticket (5 min). Differentiate for [advanced learners / struggling learners / ELL students]."

[T-02] Quiz generator:
"Create a [10/15/20]-question quiz on [topic] for [grade level] students. Include: [X] multiple choice, [X] true/false, [X] short answer questions. Vary difficulty: 30% easy, 50% medium, 20% challenging. Include an answer key."

[T-03] Rubric creator:
"Build a detailed grading rubric for [assignment type] for [grade level]. Include 4 performance levels (Exemplary/Proficient/Developing/Beginning) across these criteria: [criterion 1], [criterion 2], [criterion 3]. Make each level distinction clear and specific."

[T-04] Differentiated instruction:
"I have a lesson on [topic] for [grade level]. Create 3 versions: one for advanced learners (extension activities), one for on-grade learners (standard), one for students who need support (scaffolded). Keep the core learning objective the same."

[T-05] Parent communication:
"Write a professional email to parents explaining [situation: upcoming project / behavior concern / academic progress]. Tone: [warm but clear / direct / celebratory]. Include: what the situation is, what action is being taken, how parents can support at home, your contact info."

[T-06] IEP accommodation suggestions:
"A student has [disability/learning need]. Suggest 10 specific, practical classroom accommodations I can implement for this student in [subject] class. Focus on modifications to instruction, assessment, and environment."

[T-07] Exit ticket creator:
"Create 5 different exit ticket formats for a lesson on [topic] for [grade level]. Include: one multiple choice, one short answer, one reflection prompt, one self-assessment, one 3-2-1 style. Each should take under 3 minutes to complete."

[T-08] Project-based learning design:
"Design a project-based learning unit for [subject] on [topic] for [grade level]. Duration: [X weeks]. Include: driving question, final product/deliverable, milestones, assessment criteria, materials needed, real-world connection."

[T-09] Discussion question generator:
"Generate 15 discussion questions for [book/topic/historical event] for [grade level] students. Include: 5 recall/comprehension questions, 5 analysis questions, 5 higher-order thinking/evaluation questions. Questions should encourage debate and multiple perspectives."

[T-10] Behavior/classroom management script:
"Write a professional, calm script for addressing [specific behavior: off-task behavior / disrespect / conflict between students] in a [grade level] classroom. The script should de-escalate, preserve student dignity, and redirect to learning. Include what to say, body language notes, and next steps if behavior continues."

[T-11] Read-aloud guide: "Create a read-aloud guide for [book title] for [grade level]. Include: pre-reading vocabulary (10 words with definitions), stopping points for discussion, comprehension check questions, connections to [subject/theme/standard]."

[T-12] Student feedback script: "Write personalized, specific feedback for a student who wrote: [paste student work]. Focus on: one strong element, one specific area for improvement, and one question to push their thinking further. Tone: encouraging and growth-oriented."

[T-13] Cross-curricular connections: "I'm teaching [topic] in [subject]. Generate 5 specific connections to [other subject]. Include one hands-on activity for each connection that reinforces both subjects."

[T-14] Assessment alternative: "I typically assess [topic] with a written test. Suggest 5 alternative assessment options that measure the same standards but offer different modalities. Include: performance task, portfolio, verbal assessment, creative product, digital option."

[T-15] Classroom newsletter: "Write a weekly classroom newsletter for parents of [grade level] students. This week: [topics covered], [upcoming events], [reminder about X]. Tone: warm, brief, informative. Length: half page max."

[CC-02] Powerful coaching questions (by topic):
"Generate 20 powerful coaching questions for a client stuck on [topic: career transition / relationship conflict / business stagnation / self-confidence]. Questions should: open exploration, challenge assumptions, evoke awareness, not lead the client. Use open-ended format."

[CC-03] Client intake form: "Design a comprehensive client intake form for a [type] coach. Include: background, goals, current challenges, what they've tried, values, ideal outcome, communication preferences, schedule, and any relevant history. 15-20 questions max."

[CC-04] Proposal for consulting engagement: "Write a consulting proposal for [company type] on the project of [challenge to solve]. Include: executive summary, problem statement, proposed approach, scope, methodology, timeline, investment, team/credentials, next steps."

[CC-05] Diagnostic questionnaire: "Create a business diagnostic questionnaire for [type of consulting: marketing / operations / sales / culture]. 25 questions that reveal where the real problems are. Mix of: rating scale (1-10), yes/no, and open-ended. Group by area."

[CC-06] Client breakthrough script: "My client said: '[quote from client].' They seem stuck. Generate 5 powerful coaching interventions I can use to help them move past this block — each a different approach (reframing, direct challenge, visualization, values clarification, somatic awareness)."

[CC-07] Workshop design: "Design a half-day workshop on [topic] for [audience: executives / managers / entrepreneurs / [specific niche]]. Include: opening activity, learning objectives, 3-4 main modules with activities, debrief questions, takeaways, evaluation form."

[CC-08] Thought leadership content: "I'm a [type] consultant who helps [audience] with [outcome]. Generate a thought leadership article outline on [topic]. Include: provocative headline, key argument, supporting points, real examples, counterarguments I address, and call to action."

[CC-09] Follow-up between sessions: "Write a between-session check-in message to a client who committed to [action item] last week. Tone: encouraging, not checking up. Remind them of their commitment, offer support if stuck, celebrate forward movement."

[CC-10] Objection handling (sales calls): "I'm selling [coaching/consulting offer] at $[price]. Common objection: '[specific objection].' Write 3 different ways to respond — one logical, one emotional, one reframing. Each response should feel natural in conversation, not scripted."
