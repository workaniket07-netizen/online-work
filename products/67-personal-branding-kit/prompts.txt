BIOS BY LENGTH:

ONE-LINE BIO (Twitter/Instagram):
[BL-01] "[Title/expertise] | [Specific outcome] | [Personality or CTA]"
[BL-02] "I help [audience] [achieve outcome] | [Platform/company] | [Personal fact]"
[BL-03] "[What you do] + [who you help] + [unusual fact]"

PARAGRAPH BIO (100-150 words):
[BM-01] Professional format: credentials + experience + achievement + who you help + CTA
[BM-02] Story format: personal journey + transformation + mission + who you help
[BM-03] Value-first format: what you offer + proof + why you're different + personal human element

LONG BIO (300-500 words):
[BL-01] Full story arc: context → journey → what you know now → who you serve → vision
[BL-02] Achievement-led: notable work first → background → philosophy → current focus

BIOS BY CONTEXT:
[BC-01] Speaking bio (delivered by MC): Third-person, credentials first, memorable ending
[BC-02] Media/press bio: Most impressive achievements first
[BC-03] Website about page: Most personal version, relationship-building
[BC-04] LinkedIn summary: Mix of professional + personality + CTA

CHATGPT PROMPTS FOR BIOS (10):
[CP-01] "Write a 100-word professional bio for [name] who [role/expertise]. Key achievements: [list]. Who they help: [audience]. Include: credentials, specific results, and a human element. Third person."
[CP-02] "Rewrite this bio to be less generic and more memorable: [PASTE BIO]. Focus on: what makes this person unique, specific results, and compelling voice."
[CP-03 to CP-10] More bio prompts

[CP-03] Pillar 3: Your story/journey (personal connection)

AUTHORITY CONTENT FORMATS:
[AF-01] "Lessons from [X years/cases/clients]" — credibility through experience
[AF-02] "[Controversial topic] — my honest take" — intellectual courage
[AF-03] "A behind-the-scenes of [your work]" — process reveals expertise
[AF-04] "What I wish I'd known when starting [field]" — relatable + wise
[AF-05] "The [topic] question everyone asks me" — positions as expert

Week 1: Education (2) + Story (1) + Engagement (1) + Promotion (1)
Week 2-4: Same rotation
Monthly: 8 educational + 4 story + 4 engagement + 4 promotional + 10 conversational
