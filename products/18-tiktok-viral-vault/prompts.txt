HOW THE TIKTOK ALGORITHM WORKS (2025):

WHAT IT MEASURES (in order of importance):
1. Watch time % — Did people watch to the end? (Most important)
2. Rewatches — Did people watch again?
3. Comments — Did people take time to type?
4. Saves — Did people find it worth keeping?
5. Shares — Did people send it to others?
6. Likes — (Less important than above but still matters)

HOW TO ENGINEER EACH METRIC:

WATCH TIME:
→ Hook in first 0-1 second (no intro, no "hey guys")
→ Pattern interrupt every 5-10 seconds (cut, text, zoom, new visual)
→ Promise something early and deliver it late
→ End with a question that makes them rewatch for the answer

REWATCHES:
→ Put important information in the middle, not just at the end
→ Speak fast — people rewatch to catch what they missed
→ Pack in so much value that one watch feels incomplete

COMMENTS:
→ Ask a specific question (not "what do you think?" — too vague)
→ Create controversy — people can't resist defending their position
→ Give a binary choice (A or B, agree or disagree)
→ Call out "who this is for" — the right people will confirm in comments

SAVES:
→ Create reference content (lists, formulas, cheat sheets)
→ End with "save this for when you need it"
→ Templates, frameworks, step-by-step guides = high save rate

SHARES:
→ Make people look smart when they share it
→ "Tag someone who needs this" triggers shares
→ Content that validates someone's existing belief gets shared
→ Genuinely useful / funny / surprising content gets shared organically

POSTING FREQUENCY:
New account: 1-3x/day for first 30 days to find what works
Established: 1x/day minimum to stay in algorithm
Minimum to grow: 4x/week

BEST POSTING TIMES (general — test for your audience):
TikTok: 7-9 AM, 12-3 PM, 7-9 PM (local time)
Instagram: 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 5-7 PM
YouTube Shorts: 12-4 PM, 8-11 PM

SECTION 7: VIRAL VIDEO CHECKLIST

BEFORE POSTING — CHECK EVERY VIDEO:

HOOK (0-3 seconds):
□ Does it immediately grab attention?
□ Does it create curiosity, emotion, or immediate value?
□ No "hey guys" or slow intro?
□ Would you scroll past it? If yes, re-record.

CONTENT:
□ Every sentence moves the video forward — no filler?
□ Pattern interrupt every 5-10 seconds?
□ Clear, valuable, specific content?
□ Does it deliver on the hook's promise?

ENDING:
□ Clear CTA (comment this, save this, follow for more, link in bio)?
□ Ends at the right time — not dragging?

TECHNICAL:
□ Good lighting (ring light or natural)?
□ Clear audio (no background noise)?
□ Vertical format (9:16)?
□ Captions on (80% of views are silent)?

CAPTION + HASHTAGS:
□ Caption adds context or engagement trigger?
□ 3-7 relevant hashtags?
□ First comment ready with additional context?

POST-PUBLISH (first 30 minutes = critical):
□ Reply to first 10 comments quickly?
□ Share to your own story?
□ Pin a comment to guide conversation?
