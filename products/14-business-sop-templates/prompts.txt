SOP TITLE: Full-Cycle Recruiting Process
VERSION: 1.0 | OWNER: [HR Manager / Founder] | LAST UPDATED: [Date]
PURPOSE: Ensure consistent, fast, bias-reduced hiring for all open roles.

PROCESS OVERVIEW:
Step 1: Role Definition (Day 1-2)
Step 2: Job Posting (Day 3)
Step 3: Resume Screening (Ongoing, Day 3-14)
Step 4: Phone Screen (Day 7-14)
Step 5: Skills/Take-home Assessment (Day 10-17)
Step 6: Panel Interview (Day 14-21)
Step 7: Reference Checks (Day 18-21)
Step 8: Offer (Day 21-25)
Step 9: Onboarding Prep (Day 25-30)

DETAILED STEPS:

STEP 1 — ROLE DEFINITION
Trigger: Approved headcount request OR critical vacancy
Owner: Hiring Manager
Inputs needed: Role title, team, level, reporting structure, budget

Checklist:
□ Write job description using [Job Description Template]
□ Define must-have vs nice-to-have qualifications
□ Set compensation range (use [Salary Band Reference])
□ Define success metrics for 30/60/90 days
□ Identify 3-4 panel interviewers + their focus areas
□ Set timeline and target start date
□ Get sign-off from [Finance / Leadership]

Output: Approved job description + hiring brief

STEP 2 — JOB POSTING
Owner: HR / Recruiting
Channels to post: [LinkedIn / Indeed / company website / niche job boards]
Posting checklist:
□ Remove biased language (tool: Textio or manual review)
□ Include EEO statement
□ List compensation range (required in [states/countries where required])
□ Set application close date or "until filled"

STEP 3 — RESUME SCREENING
Screening criteria (fill per role):
Must-have #1: [criterion]
Must-have #2: [criterion]
Must-have #3: [criterion]
Disqualifiers: [list automatic disqualifiers if any]

Screen to 10-15 candidates per opening.
Record decision and reasoning for each candidate reviewed.

STEP 4 — PHONE SCREEN (30 minutes)
Questions to ask:
1. Can you tell me about your background and what you're looking for?
2. What interests you in this specific role/company?
3. [Role-specific question 1]
4. [Role-specific question 2]
5. What are your compensation expectations?
6. What's your timeline / availability to start?

Scoring rubric: Rate 1-5 on communication, relevance, enthusiasm, compensation fit
Advance: Score 4+ overall, no disqualifiers

STEP 5 — SKILLS ASSESSMENT
Options by role type:
Creative: Portfolio review checklist + 1 real-world exercise
Technical: [Take-home test / live coding / case study]
Management: Written case study
Sales: Role play scenario
Admin/Ops: Practical task test

Time limit: Maximum [2 hours] for any assessment
Compensation policy: [Paid / Unpaid — note your company policy]

STEP 6 — PANEL INTERVIEW (60-90 minutes)
Structure:
- Welcome + agenda (5 min)
- [Interviewer 1] — competency focus: [skills/experience] (20 min)
- [Interviewer 2] — competency focus: [problem-solving/analytical] (20 min)
- [Interviewer 3] — competency focus: [culture/values] (15 min)
- Candidate questions (10-15 min)
- Wrap-up + next steps (5 min)

Debrief protocol:
- Individual scores submitted BEFORE group discussion
- Discuss 30 min after last interview
- Document: strengths, concerns, decision, rationale

STEP 7 — REFERENCE CHECKS
When: After panel, before offer
Who checks: [HR / Hiring Manager]
Minimum references: 2 professional (supervisor preferred)
Questions:
1. How long and in what capacity did you work with [candidate]?
2. What were their primary responsibilities?
3. What are their greatest strengths?
4. What areas did they work to improve?
5. How did they handle [pressure/conflict/feedback]?
6. Would you rehire them? If not, why?
7. Is there anything I should know as I make my decision?

STEP 8 — OFFER
Components: Title, compensation, benefits, start date, contingencies
Verbal offer → written offer within 24 hours
Offer letter template: [See Offer Letter Template]
Exploding offer window: [X business days]
Counter-offer policy: [Your policy]

STEP 9 — ONBOARDING PREP
□ IT: Laptop + accounts + access provisioned
□ Office/workspace: Desk, badge, parking if needed
□ Announcement: Internal team email drafted
□ Day 1 schedule: First week agenda created
□ Buddy/mentor: Assigned
□ 30/60/90 plan: Draft ready

SOP: SALES PROCESS — B2B SERVICE BUSINESS

TITLE: B2B Sales Process
PURPOSE: Convert leads to clients consistently and predictably.

STAGE 1 — LEAD QUALIFICATION
Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) criteria:
□ Company size: [X-Y employees / $X-Y revenue]
□ Industry: [list]
□ Challenge: [problem your service solves]
□ Budget indicator: [signals they have budget]
□ Decision-maker access: [role of decision-maker]
□ Timeline: [actively looking to buy in X timeframe]

Lead scoring: 1 point per ICP criterion met. Score 4+: Priority 1. Score 2-3: Nurture. Score 0-1: Not a fit.

STAGE 2 — FIRST OUTREACH
Medium: [email / LinkedIn / referral / inbound]
Goal: Book discovery call
Message framework: [observation about them] + [relevant connection] + [soft ask for call]
Follow-up cadence:
Day 1: First outreach
Day 4: Follow-up 1 (add value)
Day 9: Follow-up 2 (softer touch)
Day 16: Final attempt + breakup email
If no response: Tag for 90-day re-engage sequence

STAGE 3 — DISCOVERY CALL (30-45 minutes)
Pre-call prep:
□ Research company (news, team size, recent announcements)
□ Prepare 5 discovery questions specific to their situation
□ Review ICP criteria

Call framework:
Opening (3 min): Context + agenda
Discovery (20 min): [List your 5-8 core discovery questions]
Presentation (10 min): High-level overview of approach + relevant examples
Qualification (5 min): Budget, timeline, decision process
Next steps (5 min): Proposal / follow-up call / decision criteria

Qualify: BANT (Budget / Authority / Need / Timeline) or MEDDIC
Advance if: Clear need + timeline + decision maker involved + budget range aligned

STAGE 4 — PROPOSAL
Trigger: Qualified discovery call completed
Turnaround: Within 48 hours of call
Template: [See Proposal Template]
Format: PDF or online doc
Follow-up: Call 48 hours after sending to review together
Proposal expiry: 30 days

STAGE 5 — NEGOTIATION & CLOSE
Never discount without getting something in return
Options for budget objection:
→ Reduce scope (not rate)
→ Phase the project
→ Payment plan
→ Shorter initial engagement to prove value

Verbal agreement → contract within 24 hours
Contract tool: [DocuSign / HelloSign / your tool]
Deposit invoice: Send simultaneously with contract

STAGE 6 — HANDOFF TO DELIVERY
Once signed + deposit received:
□ Project folder created
□ Kickoff call scheduled within 48 hours
□ Client added to [CRM / project management tool]
□ Delivery team briefed
□ Kickoff agenda sent to client

SOP: CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING

TITLE: Customer Complaint Resolution Process
PURPOSE: Turn complaints into retention opportunities.

RECEIPT (within 1 hour):
□ Acknowledge receipt: "Thank you for reaching out. I'm looking into this now."
□ Log in [CRM/ticket system]
□ Assign owner
□ Set resolution target: [4 hours / 24 hours / 48 hours depending on severity]

SEVERITY LEVELS:
Level 1 (Minor): Wrong information, billing question → Resolve same day
Level 2 (Medium): Service issue, delay, quality concern → Resolve within 24 hours
Level 3 (Major): Service failure, significant client impact → Escalate immediately, resolve within 4 hours

RESOLUTION PROCESS:
1. Understand fully: Ask clarifying questions before defending
2. Acknowledge: "I understand why that's frustrating."
3. Own: Take responsibility without excessive blame
4. Fix: Tell them exactly what you'll do and when
5. Follow up: Check that the resolution was satisfactory
6. Document: Log root cause and fix to prevent recurrence

RESPONSE TEMPLATES BY ISSUE TYPE:
Billing error: [template]
Quality issue: [template]
Delay: [template]
Miscommunication: [template]
Complete failure: [template]
Refund request: [template]

ESCALATION CRITERIA: Involve manager when:
→ Client threatens to cancel
→ Legal implications
→ Media/social threat
→ Issue affects multiple clients
→ Level 3 complaint

TITLE: Client Project Delivery Process
PURPOSE: Deliver every project on time, on scope, to a quality standard clients rave about.

KICKOFF PHASE (Week 1)
□ Send kickoff agenda 24 hours in advance
□ Kickoff call: Review scope, timeline, communication plan, first milestone
□ Set up project workspace ([Notion/Asana/ClickUp/your tool])
□ Share access with client
□ Send kickoff summary email within 24 hours of call
□ Begin work immediately — no "setup lag"

COMMUNICATION STANDARDS:
→ Response time: Within [4/8/24] hours on business days
→ Status updates: Every [Friday / week / milestone]
→ Method: [Email / Slack / your tool] for [category of communication]
→ Escalation: If blocked > 24 hours, proactively communicate
