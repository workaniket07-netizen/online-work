BIO WRITING PRINCIPLES (read first)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What WORKS in dating bios:
→ Specific details (not "I love to travel" — say where you've been)
→ Showing personality through humor or wit
→ Being selective — quality over quantity of info
→ A conversation hook (give them something to respond to)
→ Light vulnerability or authenticity
→ What makes you different from everyone else on the app

What DOESN'T work:
→ Generic: "I love to laugh," "I'm an open book," "looking for my partner in crime"
→ Negative: listing what you DON'T want
→ Passive: "message me if you want to know more"
→ Braggy: dropping achievements without context
→ Too long: wall of text = swipe left

THE BIO FORMULA:
Hook (1-2 sentences) + Personality reveal (2-3 sentences) + One interesting specific detail + Conversation invitation

MALE PROFILE BIO TEMPLATES (20)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[MB-01] THE WIT + SUBSTANCE BIO:
"I have strong opinions about [specific thing everyone thinks they know — like coffee or running routes]. Professionally: [brief description without jargon]. Non-professionally: I've been to [X countries], make [something you cook] from scratch, and recently started [interesting hobby or project]. Here for [what you're actually looking for — be honest]. Ask me about [conversation starter]."

[MB-02] THE STORY BIO:
"Last year I [did something genuine and interesting — took a solo trip, learned a skill, made a decision you're proud of]. Before that I was [what you used to be/do], so it was a shift. I'm [how you'd describe yourself in 3 words] — depending on the day, [3 different words]. Looking to meet someone who [specific quality you genuinely value]."

[MB-03] THE CONFIDENT + DIRECT BIO:
"[Your name]. [Job/what you do] by day, [what you do otherwise] by night. I'm [2-3 specific honest descriptors — not generic]. Passionate about [genuine interest with specificity]. My friends would say I'm [specific quality]. Looking for someone who [specific — not 'is honest and kind']."

[MB-04] THE HUMOR-FIRST BIO:
"[Funny opener based on a universal experience or light self-deprecation — must be genuine]. On a more serious note: [2-3 real things about you]. The only [app cliché] I'll make: I'm [something playful + real]. Looking for someone who can [specific quality you find attractive]."

[MB-05] THE SHORT & SPECIFIC:
"Work: [X]. Currently obsessed with: [very specific thing]. Weekend version of me: [2 activities]. Dealbreaker: [one light/specific/honest one].

Looking for: someone I can [do specific thing] with and still [do other specific thing] with."

[MB-06] THE INTELLECTUAL/CURIOUS BIO:
"I read [genre] mostly, watch [type of content] occasionally, and have been genuinely thinking about [interesting question or observation]. I'm [your field/work in plain language]. Outside of that: [2-3 specifics]. Would love to meet someone who takes [interest] as seriously as I take [your thing], but probably knows more about [something you want to learn]."

THE OPENING MESSAGE FORMULA:
What works: Specific reference to their profile + genuine reaction + easy-to-answer question or comment
What doesn't: "Hey" / "You're beautiful" / copy-paste messages / too much too fast

[OM-08] They seem adventurous: "Your bio gives off [adventurous person] vibes. Scale of 1-10 — how spontaneous are you willing to get for a [first / second] date?"

[BM-01] "Hey" — no effort, easy to ignore
[BM-02] "You're so beautiful" — puts looks first, feels shallow
[BM-03] "What are you looking for?" — too serious, too soon
[BM-04] "I'm [your job description]" — nobody asked
[BM-05] Explicit compliments on body — almost always wrong
[BM-06] Anything copy-paste obvious — they can tell
[BM-07] "DTF?" or equivalent — terrible approach
[BM-08] "Tell me about yourself" — lazy, makes them do all the work
[BM-09] Negative openers — "I don't usually swipe right but..."
[BM-10] Too-long first messages — overwhelming

CONVERSATION GOALS BY STAGE:
Stage 1 (Messages 1-5): Establish rapport, find a real point of connection
Stage 2 (Messages 5-20): Build genuine interest, show your personality
Stage 3 (Messages 20+): If still going well → suggest meeting in person
Stage 4: Meeting in person
