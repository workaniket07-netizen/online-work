CREDIT SCORE EXPLAINED:
[CS-01] Five factors: Payment history (35%) | Amounts owed (30%) | Length of history (15%) | New credit (10%) | Credit mix (10%)
[CS-02] How to build credit from zero: Secured card → become authorized user → credit builder loan
[CS-03] Dispute a credit report error: Free report at annualcreditreport.com → dispute letter template → follow up
[CS-04] What hurts your score: Late payments (biggest impact) | Closing old accounts | Opening too many at once | High utilization (>30%)
[CS-05] Utilization hack: Pay before the statement closes (not just before due date) to report lower balance

APARTMENT/HOUSE LEASE GUIDE:
[AL-01] What to inspect before signing: Water pressure, outlets, heating/cooling, appliances, window seals, pests, mold
[AL-02] Lease terms to negotiate: Move-in date, rent price, pet policy, subletting, early termination
[AL-03] Utility setup checklist: Electric, gas, internet, renter's insurance (required by most landlords)
[AL-04] Renter's insurance: $127-30/month — covers your stuff (landlord's insurance doesn't)
[AL-05] Move-in inspection: Document EVERYTHING on paper and video before moving in
[AL-06] Security deposit: Get receipt, know your state's laws on return timeline (usually 14-30 days)

FIRST JOB FINANCIAL CHECKLIST:
[FJ-01] 401k: Contribute at least to employer match (free money — don't leave it)
[FJ-02] Health insurance enrollment: Compare plans — lower premium ≠ better if high deductible
[FJ-03] HSA: Pair with high-deductible plan — triple tax advantage
[FJ-04] Direct deposit: Set up split — % to savings automatically
[FJ-05] W-4: Fill correctly or face surprise tax bill

CAR MAINTENANCE BASICS:
[CM-01] Oil change: Every 5,000-7,500 miles for synthetic oil. Don't skip.
[CM-02] Tire pressure: Check monthly. Find correct PSI on driver door sticker (not tire sidewall).
[CM-03] Warning lights guide: Check engine / battery / oil / temp / brake — what each means + urgency
[CM-04] When to replace: Tires (tread penny test) | Brakes (squealing/grinding) | Wipers (streaking)
[CM-05] Car buying checklist: Pre-purchase inspection by independent mechanic, Carfax report, total cost of ownership calculation
[CM-06] Insurance: Liability required everywhere. Full coverage: if car worth >10x annual premium.

MEDICAL & HEALTH SYSTEM:
[MH-01] How insurance works: Premium + Deductible + Copay + Coinsurance + Out-of-pocket max
[MH-02] When to go where: Urgent care (non-emergency, same day) | ER (true emergency only) | PCP (regular care, referrals) | Telehealth (minor issues fast)
[MH-03] Prescription savings: GoodRx app for generics | Ask for generic version | 90-day supply = cheaper
[MH-04] Medical bill negotiation: "I can't afford this. What's your cash-pay rate?" | Ask for itemized bill | Dispute duplicates
[MH-05] Preventive care: Usually 100% covered — annual physical, vaccines, screenings. Use it.

COOKING BASICS FOR BEGINNERS:
[CK-01] The 5 techniques worth learning: Sauté, roast, boil, simmer, and bake
[CK-02] Weekly meal prep in 2 hours: Grain | Protein | Roasted vegetables | Sauce → 5 different meals from same base
[CK-03] Pantry staples that make any meal: Olive oil, garlic, onions, canned tomatoes, canned beans, pasta, rice, soy sauce, lemon
[CK-04] Recipe template for any stir-fry: Oil + aromatics (garlic/ginger) + protein + vegetables + sauce + served over grain
[CK-05] Knife skills basics: Chef's knife + cutting board + the "claw grip" = 90% of kitchen tasks

EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS:
[EP-01] Go-bag: 72-hour kit with water (1 gallon/person/day), food, medications, documents, cash, phone charger, flashlight
[EP-02] Important documents: Copies of IDs, insurance cards, bank accounts, in a waterproof container
[EP-03] Emergency contacts: Written list (not just in phone — phones die)
[EP-04] Power outage plan: Know where your circuit breaker is. Have flashlights. Know how to manually open garage.

PROFESSIONAL EMAIL TEMPLATES (20):
[PE-01] First contact with professional: "Hi [Name], my name is [X]. I'm reaching out because [specific reason]. [What you're asking / proposing in 1 sentence]. [Proposed next step]. Thank you for your time."
[PE-02] Following up: "Hi [Name], following up on my email from [date]. [One-sentence reminder of ask]. Do you have a moment this week? Thank you."
[PE-03] Saying no gracefully: "Thank you for thinking of me. I won't be able to [request] right now, but [alternative if you have one]. I appreciate you reaching out."
[PE-04] Requesting a meeting: "Hi [Name], I'd love 20 minutes to discuss [specific topic]. Are you available [day/time options]? Happy to work around your schedule."

DIFFICULT CONVERSATIONS:
[DC-01] Asking for help: "I'm working on [X] and got stuck on [specific issue]. Do you have [X minutes] to talk through it? I've already tried [what you tried]."
[DC-02] Disagreeing professionally: "I understand your perspective. I see it slightly differently: [your view]. Can we discuss both approaches?"
[DC-03] Addressing conflict: "I want to talk about [situation] because I think we have different understandings of [X]. Can we find 15 minutes?"

NETWORKING SCRIPTS (10):
[NW-01] At an event: "What brings you to [event]? What do you do? How did you get into that?" [Genuine interest > collecting cards]
[NW-02] LinkedIn connection: "[Name], I [how you know them / why you want to connect]. I'd love to connect to [specific reason]. Thanks!"
[NW-03] Informational interview ask: "Hi [Name], I'm exploring [career area] and would love to hear about your experience at [company/in field]. Would you be open to a 20-minute call?"

IMPORTANT DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST:
□ Birth certificate (original + digital copy)
□ Social Security card (store securely, not in wallet)
□ Passport (check expiry date — renew 9 months before trip)
□ Driver's license
□ Health insurance cards + policy numbers
□ Car insurance + home/renter's insurance
□ Will and/or trust (if applicable)
□ Bank account numbers + routing numbers (secure location)
□ Investment account information

INSURANCE COVERAGE CHECKLIST:
□ Health insurance: Active and current
□ Renter's or homeowner's insurance: Covers replacement value
□ Car insurance: At minimum liability required by state
□ Life insurance: If others depend on your income (often free through work)
□ Disability insurance: Protects income if you can't work (more likely than death)
□ Umbrella: If net worth > $250k

ANNUAL LIFE AUDIT (do every January):
□ Review and update beneficiaries on all accounts
□ Check credit report (annualcreditreport.com — free)
□ Review insurance coverage and premiums (shop competitors)
□ Update emergency contacts
□ Check subscription list — cancel what you don't use
□ Review will/estate documents
□ Max out tax-advantaged accounts for the year
□ Review investment allocation
