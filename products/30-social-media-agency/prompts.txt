CLIENT INTAKE QUESTIONNAIRE:
[CQ-01] Business overview: What does your business do? Who is your ideal customer?
[CQ-02] Brand voice: 3 words that describe your brand personality. Brands you admire (and why)?
[CQ-03] Current social presence: Which platforms? Follower count? Current content frequency?
[CQ-04] Goals: Primary goal for social media this year? Secondary? How do you measure success?
[CQ-05] Content history: What's worked well? What hasn't worked? What do you want to do more of?
[CQ-06] Brand assets: Do you have brand guidelines? Logo files? Photography? Color codes?
[CQ-07] Competitors: Top 3 competitors + what they do well on social?
[CQ-08] Audience: Age range, location, income level, biggest pain points?
[CQ-09] Approvals: Who approves content? What's the turnaround expected?
[CQ-10] Sensitive topics: Any topics to avoid? Political/religious/competitor mentions policy?

CLIENT COMMUNICATION SCRIPTS (20):
[CC-01] Monthly check-in: "Hi [Name], your monthly report is attached. Key highlight: [one win]. One thing I want to discuss: [one concern or opportunity]. Are you free [day] for a brief call?"

[CC-02] Handling "why aren't we growing faster": "Growth on social media compounds — the first 90 days are always the slowest. Here's where we are vs benchmark: [data]. Here's what I'm doing to accelerate: [3 specific actions next month]."

[CC-03] Upsell to higher package: "Based on [X months] of data, your account is ready for a more aggressive growth strategy. If we add [X service], here's what I expect to happen: [specific projection]. That would be $[X] more per month. Want to discuss?"

[CC-04] Difficult feedback response: "Thank you for being direct — I appreciate it. I hear [their feedback]. My honest response: [your take]. Here's what I'll change going forward: [specific actions]. Can we agree on [next metric/milestone] as our success measure?"

[CC-05] Re-engagement: "Hi [Name], I noticed engagement has been lower this month. I want to address it head-on: [explanation + plan]. Before our next call, I'd like to share a revised approach. Can we schedule [date/time]?"
