REMOTE-SPECIFIC INTERVIEW QUESTIONS + ANSWERS:

[IQ-01] "How do you stay productive when working from home?"
Framework: Environment → routine → tools → communication → boundaries
Sample: "I work from a dedicated home office, follow a structured schedule, use [tools] for deep work, and maintain clear boundaries between work and personal time. I've found that [specific habit] has been key to my productivity."

[IQ-02] "How do you communicate with a distributed team?"
Key points: proactive communication, over-document, time zone awareness, async-first, when to escalate to sync

[IQ-03] "How do you handle isolation or lack of collaboration in remote work?"
Key points: online communities, virtual coffee chats, contributing to async discussion, finding local coworking spaces

[IQ-04] "Tell me about a challenge you faced working remotely and how you solved it."
STAR format: Situation (remote challenge) → Task → Action → Result

[IQ-05] "What's your home office setup like?"
Mention: dedicated space, good lighting, quality microphone, reliable internet, backup plan
