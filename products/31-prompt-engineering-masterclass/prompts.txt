TECHNIQUE 1: ROLE / PERSONA PROMPTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What it does: Dramatically improves output quality by activating specific knowledge domains and communication styles.

BASIC FORMAT:
"You are [specific expert with credentials/experience]. [Task]."

POWER VERSIONS:
[PE-01] "You are a [senior copywriter at Apple with 15 years experience writing product launches]. I need you to [task]. Your output should sound like [Apple product launch copy — specific, benefit-focused, emotionally resonant, no jargon]."

[PE-02] "Act as a [Michelin-star chef specializing in Italian cuisine]. A customer asks: [question]. Respond with the expertise, nuance, and specificity a professional chef would use — including technique names, ingredient rationale, and common mistakes to avoid."

[PE-03] "You are a [skeptical venture capitalist who has seen 1,000 pitch decks]. Read this pitch and give me your honest assessment: [paste pitch]. Don't soften feedback. What would make you say yes vs. pass?"

[PE-04] COMBINED EXPERT PANEL: "There are three experts at the table: [Expert A], [Expert B], [Expert C]. They each respond to this question from their distinct perspective: [question]. Show their perspectives as a dialogue."

[PE-05] THE ANTI-EXPERT: "You are the worst possible [type of person] at [topic]. Give me advice the way a [bad version] would — so I can see exactly what NOT to do and why."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNIQUE 2: CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT (COT) PROMPTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What it does: Forces the AI to reason step-by-step, significantly improving accuracy on complex tasks.

BASIC COT:
"Think through this step by step before giving your final answer: [problem]"

[COT-01] EXPLICIT COT: "Before answering, work through your reasoning step by step. Show your work. Only give your conclusion after you've reasoned through it completely. Question: [question]"

[COT-02] SELF-CHECK COT: "Answer this question: [question]. Then review your answer and check: Did I miss anything? Are there any errors in my reasoning? Correct yourself if needed. Show both the original answer and the revised version."

[COT-03] DEVIL'S ADVOCATE COT: "First, make the strongest argument FOR [position]. Then make the strongest argument AGAINST [position]. Then synthesize both into a nuanced conclusion."

[COT-04] PROBLEM DECOMPOSITION: "Break this complex problem into its component parts: [problem]. Address each part separately, then synthesize your findings into a complete answer."

[COT-05] HYPOTHESIS TESTING: "I believe [hypothesis]. Walk through the evidence that supports this, evidence against it, and rate your confidence in the hypothesis on a scale of 1-10 with reasoning."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNIQUE 3: FEW-SHOT LEARNING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What it does: Provides examples so AI understands exactly the pattern/format you want.

[FS-01] THE EXAMPLE-INPUT-OUTPUT FORMAT:
"Here are examples of what I want:

Input: [example input 1]
Output: [example output 1]

Input: [example input 2]
Output: [example output 2]

Input: [example input 3]
Output: [example output 3]

Now do the same for:
Input: [your actual input]
Output:"

[FS-02] STYLE MIMICRY: "Read these [X] pieces of writing by [author/brand]: [samples]. Now write [new piece] in the exact same voice, sentence structure, and style."

[FS-03] FORMAT LEARNING: "Here's the exact format I want:
[Show complete example with every field filled in]
Now apply this format to: [your actual data/content]"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNIQUE 4: OUTPUT FORMATTING CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[OF-01] JSON OUTPUT: "Return your response as a JSON object with these fields: {field1: value, field2: value, field3: value}. Only output the JSON, no other text."

[OF-02] TABLE FORMAT: "Present this information as a markdown table with columns: [col1], [col2], [col3]. Sort by [column] descending."

[OF-03] STRICT WORD COUNT: "Write this in exactly [X] words. No more, no less. If your first draft is too long or short, revise until it hits exactly [X] words."

[OF-04] NUMBERED HIERARCHY: "Organize your response as: 1. [Main point] a. [Sub-point] b. [Sub-point] i. [Details]. Two levels deep maximum."

[OF-05] TEMPLATE FILL: "Fill in this template exactly: [Template with [FIELD] placeholders]. Do not change the structure, only fill in the [FIELD] sections."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNIQUE 5: CONSTRAINT PROMPTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[CP-01] AUDIENCE CONSTRAINT: "Explain [concept] to a [8-year-old / non-technical executive / expert in the field]. Use vocabulary appropriate for that audience only."

[CP-02] CONSTRAINT STACKING: "Write [X] about [topic]. CONSTRAINTS: Under [Y] words. No jargon. No passive voice. No filler words. Every sentence must do one of: teach, intrigue, or advance the reader."

[CP-03] FORCED PERSPECTIVE: "Explain [topic] from the perspective of someone who [hates it / is a beginner / is in 1950 / is from Mars]. Keep the explanation accurate but through that lens."

[CP-04] ELIMINATION: "Rewrite this without: [specific words to avoid] / [passive voice] / [any hedging language] / [adjectives]. [paste original]"

[CP-05] THE ELEVATOR TEST: "Can you explain [complex concept] in under 30 seconds? Write it in plain language as if you had to say it in an elevator to your CEO."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNIQUE 6: ITERATIVE REFINEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[IR-01] THE CRITIC LOOP: "Write [X]. Now critique it as a [relevant expert]. Now rewrite based on the critique. Do this [3] times."

[IR-02] THE UPGRADE PROMPT: "This output is a 7/10. What would make it a 10/10? Rewrite it based on your own critique."

[IR-03] SPECIFICITY INJECTION: "This response is too generic. Make it [X]% more specific by: adding a concrete example, a statistic, and a named technique. Rewrite: [paste response]"

[IR-04] THE CALIBRATION PROMPT: "On a scale of 1-10, how [confident/accurate/complete] is your answer to [question]? What would make it higher? Now revise until you'd rate it an 8+."

[IR-05] BUILD-ON: "Good, now take that answer and [add counterarguments / make it more actionable / add a visual description / apply it to [specific industry]]."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNIQUE 7: METACOGNITIVE PROMPTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[MC-01] "Before answering, identify: What type of question is this? What knowledge domains are relevant? What biases might affect your response? Then answer with those considerations in mind."

[MC-02] "What are the most important things you DON'T know about [topic] that would affect the quality of your answer? State those limitations first, then give your best answer."

[MC-03] "What questions should I be asking about [topic] that I'm not asking? List them, then answer the most important ones."

[MC-04] "What assumptions am I making in this question: [question]? Are those assumptions correct? How does changing them change the answer?"

[MC-05] "What would [different types of experts] each say is wrong with my approach to [problem]? List 3 different expert perspectives."

These prompts do what entire SaaS tools charge $127-500/month for:

[PP-01] THE BUSINESS ANALYZER (replaces business consultants):
"Analyze [business name/description] across these dimensions: Value proposition clarity, Target market definition, Revenue model viability, Competitive moat, Growth levers, Key risks, One highest-priority recommendation. Use data and reasoning, not generic advice."

[PP-02] THE MARKETING STRATEGY GENERATOR (replaces marketing agencies):
"Create a 90-day marketing strategy for [business description] targeting [audience]. Include: positioning statement, top 3 channels with rationale, 30/60/90-day milestones, budget allocation framework, KPIs, and what success looks like. Be specific to this business, not generic."

[PP-03] THE CODE REVIEWER (replaces senior dev review):
"Review this [language] code as a senior engineer. Check for: bugs, security vulnerabilities, performance issues, scalability concerns, code readability, documentation gaps, test coverage. Prioritize by severity. Be specific — cite line numbers. [paste code]"

[PP-04] THE LEGAL PLAIN ENGLISH TRANSLATOR (not legal advice):
"Translate this [contract/terms of service/agreement] into plain English. For each clause: what it means, what it allows, what it restricts, and any potential risks. Flag any unusual or potentially problematic clauses. [paste document]"

[PP-05] THE INVESTOR PITCH RATER (replaces pitch coaches):
"Rate this pitch deck as a skeptical VC who has seen 1,000 pitches. Score each section 1-10: Problem, Solution, Market size, Business model, Traction, Team, Ask. Give specific feedback on what's missing, what's unclear, and what would make you want a second meeting. [paste pitch or description]"

[PP-06] THE RESEARCH SYNTHESIZER:
"I have these [X] sources on [topic]: [paste or describe]. Synthesize the key findings. Where do they agree? Where do they contradict? What conclusions can I draw? What questions remain unanswered? Give me a structured summary."

[PP-07] THE CONTENT REPURPOSER (replaces content tools):
"Take this [blog post/video transcript/podcast episode]: [paste]. Repurpose it into: 1 LinkedIn post, 1 Twitter/X thread (10 tweets), 1 email newsletter, 5 Instagram captions, 3 short video scripts (30 seconds each). Keep the core message, adapt format for each platform."

[PP-08] THE NEGOTIATION COACH:
"I need to negotiate [situation: salary / contract / price / partnership]. My position: [your position]. Their likely position: [their side]. Prepare me: best opening offer, key points to make, likely objections and responses, BATNA, and the concessions I can make vs. lines I shouldn't cross."

[PP-09] THE COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE REPORT:
"Analyze [company/brand] as a competitor. Based on what's publicly known: their positioning, strengths, weaknesses, target customer, pricing strategy, content strategy, likely next moves, and where they're vulnerable. What should I do based on this?"

[PP-10] THE PRODUCT FEEDBACK ANALYZER:
"Here are [X] customer reviews of [product]: [paste reviews]. Identify: top 5 praise themes, top 5 complaint themes, unmet needs customers keep mentioning, one feature request that appears most frequently, and what the ideal product/service would look like based on this feedback."

Always write copy that: sells benefits not features, uses specific language, includes social proof signals, has a clear CTA. When I ask for copy, start writing immediately — don't ask clarifying questions unless the task is genuinely ambiguous."

[CI-02] Research assistant instructions: "You are a research assistant. When I ask research questions: cite your confidence level, acknowledge limitations of your knowledge, distinguish between well-established facts and uncertain information, and suggest where I should verify key claims."

SYSTEM PROMPT TEMPLATES (20):
[SP-01] Customer service bot: "You are [Company] customer service. Tone: [warm, professional]. You can: [list capabilities]. You cannot: [list limitations]. If asked about [sensitive topic], respond: [response]. Always end with: 'Is there anything else I can help with?'"

[SP-02] Sales qualification bot: "You are [Company] pre-sales assistant. Your job: qualify leads by asking about [budget / timeline / current solution / decision-making]. After [X questions], categorize the lead as [Tier 1/2/3] and respond accordingly. Never discuss pricing — direct pricing questions to a human."

[SP-03 to SP-20] More system prompts: HR FAQ bot, educational tutor, brand voice guardian, onboarding guide, internal knowledge base assistant

MULTI-TURN CONVERSATION TECHNIQUES (20):
[MT-01] THE MEMORY HACK: "At the start of each session, paste: 'Context for this session: [your project/context]. My preferences: [style/format preferences]. Continue from where we left off.'"

[MT-02] PROGRESSIVE REFINEMENT: Start broad → narrow with each turn. "Give me 20 ideas → Now narrow to the 5 best → Now develop the top choice in detail."

[MT-03] SOCRATIC DIALOGUE: Ask AI to question your thinking. "Push back on everything I say about [topic]. Ask me 'why' and 'how do you know' for every claim."

[MT-04 to MT-20] More multi-turn techniques
