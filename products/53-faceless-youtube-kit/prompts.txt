SCRIPT TEMPLATE — FACELESS STYLE:

[SC-01] PERSONAL FINANCE VIDEO SCRIPT (8-12 minutes):

HOOK (0:00-0:20):
"If you have $[AMOUNT] in a savings account right now, you're making a massive mistake. And no, this isn't clickbait. In the next [X] minutes, I'll show you exactly what you should be doing instead — and why most people get this completely wrong."

INTRO (0:20-1:00):
"Before we dive in, let me be clear — this isn't investment advice. What I'm sharing is educational information about how money actually works. And once you understand it, you'll never look at a savings account the same way.

Let's start with a number: [SHOCKING STATISTIC about inflation/savings/opportunity cost]."

BODY SECTION 1 (1:00-4:00):
"Here's the problem most people don't realize: [EXPLANATION WITH DATA AND EXAMPLES]

Think about it this way: [ANALOGY]

The math is actually pretty simple: [CALCULATION EXAMPLE]"

BODY SECTION 2 (4:00-7:00):
"So what should you do instead? Let me walk you through [NUMBER] options, starting with the easiest.

Option 1: [OPTION WITH SPECIFIC DETAILS]
Option 2: [OPTION WITH PROS/CONS]
Option 3: [OPTION — HIGHER RETURN/MORE EFFORT]"

BODY SECTION 3 (7:00-10:00):
"Here's what the data shows about people who [DID WHAT YOU'RE RECOMMENDING]:
[STATS AND REAL EXAMPLES]"

CLOSE (10:00-11:00):
"Let me summarize what we covered: [3 BULLET POINTS]

The action step I'd recommend today: [SPECIFIC FIRST STEP]

If this was helpful, subscribe — I post videos like this every [FREQUENCY] about [CHANNEL TOPIC]. See you in the next one."

---

Hook → Dramatic historical moment → Context → Deeper story → Significance today → Call to action

[SC-03] MOTIVATIONAL / SELF-IMPROVEMENT:

[SC-04] TRUE CRIME / MYSTERY:

[SC-05] TECH TUTORIAL (SCREENCAST):
Hook (problem you solve) → Overview → Step 1 (detailed) → Step 2 → ... → Result → Additional tips → Subscribe CTA
