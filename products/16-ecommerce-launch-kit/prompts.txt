THE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION FRAMEWORK:
1. Hook (emotional — problem or aspiration)
2. Introduce the product (clear, specific)
3. Key benefits (not just features)
4. Feature list (scannable bullets)
5. Social proof indicator
6. CTA + specifics (size, material, shipping)

---

[PD-01] STANDARD PRODUCT DESCRIPTION TEMPLATE

[PRODUCT NAME]

[HOOK: 1-2 sentences addressing the problem this product solves or the feeling it creates]
Example: "Finding the right [product type] shouldn't feel like a compromise. [Product Name] gives you [main benefit] without [common sacrifice]."

[INTRO: What it is and who it's for]
"[Product Name] is [brief description] designed for [target customer] who [specific need/desire]."

[KEY BENEFITS — write these before features]:
• [Benefit 1 — how it makes their life better]: [Feature that enables it]
• [Benefit 2 — outcome they get]: [Feature that enables it]
• [Benefit 3 — problem it solves]: [Feature that enables it]

[FEATURE LIST]:
✓ [Specific feature + specification]
✓ [Specific feature + specification]
✓ [Specific feature + specification]
✓ [Material/quality detail]
✓ [Size/dimension/compatibility]
✓ [What's included]

[SOCIAL PROOF SEED]:
"Trusted by [number] customers / Rated [X]/5 by verified buyers"
OR: "[Specific use case quote or scenario]"

[CTA]:
Available in: [colors/sizes/variations]
Shipping: [time frame] | Returns: [policy]
[Buy Now / Add to Cart] — [urgency element if applicable]

[PD-31 to PD-100] FEATURE-TO-BENEFIT CONVERSION FORMULAS

The most powerful thing in e-commerce copy: translating features into benefits.

FORMULA: "[Feature] means [benefit] so you can [outcome]"

EXAMPLES BY CATEGORY:

APPAREL:
Feature: "Moisture-wicking fabric"
Benefit: "Keeps you dry even on your hardest runs"
Copy: "Moisture-wicking fabric keeps you dry even on your hardest runs — so you can focus on the mile ahead, not the discomfort."

Feature: "4-way stretch"
Benefit: "Moves with you in every direction"
Copy: "4-way stretch moves with you — whether you're sprinting, stretching, or sitting in back-to-back meetings."

ELECTRONICS:
Feature: "6-hour battery life"
Benefit: "Get through your full workday on one charge"
Copy: "6-hour battery life means you get through your full workday — meeting to meeting — without hunting for an outlet."

Feature: "Noise-canceling"
Benefit: "Work without distractions anywhere"
Copy: "Noise-canceling technology creates your own quiet zone — on the train, in the cafe, in the open office."

SKINCARE:
Feature: "Hyaluronic acid formula"
Benefit: "Deep hydration that lasts all day"
Copy: "Hyaluronic acid draws moisture from the air into your skin — keeping it plump and hydrated from morning to night."

FOOD:
Feature: "Cold-pressed"
Benefit: "Maximum nutrients, no compromise"
Copy: "Cold-pressed at 35°F preserves up to 3x more nutrients than heat processing — so every sip actually does something."

[Paint the after picture — specific, desirable outcome]

[Offer + CTA]"
