CLASSROOM PROCEDURES HANDBOOK TEMPLATE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WELCOME TO [MR./MS./MRS.] [NAME]'S CLASS

Room number: [X] | Grade: [X] | Subject: [X]

HOW WE ENTER CLASS:
1. Enter quietly and go directly to your seat
2. Check the board for today's warm-up activity
3. Begin the warm-up immediately — it lasts [X] minutes
4. Materials should be on your desk: [list]

HOW WE TRANSITION:
→ When I say "[signal]", stop all activity within 5 seconds
→ Eyes forward, voice off, materials down
→ Wait for instructions before moving

WHEN YOU NEED SOMETHING:
→ During instruction: raise your hand silently
→ Water/restroom: use the sign in the room (one at a time)
→ Supplies: available at [location] — get what you need during transitions only
→ Emergency: say "[code word]" and I will respond immediately

ENTERING LATE:
→ Sign in at the door
→ Silently take your seat
→ Check with a neighbor about what you missed — not during instruction

HOW WE WORK IN GROUPS:
→ Voice level: [1/2/3 scale — explain your scale]
→ Stay in your group space
→ If you need help: ask your group first, then raise your hand
→ Every group member contributes

HOW WE TURN IN WORK:
→ Physical work: [basket/location/method]
→ Digital work: [platform/where to submit]
→ Late work: [your policy, stated clearly]

HOW WE LEAVE:
→ Wait for dismissal — I dismiss, not the bell
→ Leave your area cleaner than you found it
→ Push in chair, pick up any floor trash

BEHAVIOR MANAGEMENT SCRIPTS (20):
[BM-01] Private redirection: "Hey [Name] — [brief, quiet, specific]. I need [behavior]. Can you do that?"
[BM-02] Public redirection (minimal attention): "[Name], [specific behavior change], please." (move on immediately)
[BM-03] The choice: "You have two choices: [option A] or [option B]. Which do you choose?"
[BM-04] Acknowledging effort: "I noticed you [specific positive behavior]. Thank you."
[BM-05] The cool-down: "I can see you're frustrated. Let's take a moment before we continue."
[BM-06] The warning: "This is a reminder about [behavior]. If it continues, [consequence]. I know you can make a better choice."
[BM-07] The consequence delivery: "[Name], because of [behavior], [consequence]. I'll see you after class to discuss."
[BM-08] The after-class conference: "I'm not here to get you in trouble. I'm here to help you succeed. What was happening for you today?"
[BM-09] Disruptive behavior during instruction: "I'm going to pause for a moment. [Name], what do you need right now to be successful?"
[BM-10] Phone confiscation script: "I need your phone for the period. You'll get it back at [time]. This isn't a punishment — it's our classroom agreement."

PARENT COMMUNICATION TEMPLATES (30):
[PC-01] WEEKLY NEWSLETTER FORMAT:
Subject: [Classroom Name] Weekly Update — Week of [Date]

Hello Families,

This week in [class/subject]:
→ We learned: [brief description]
→ We practiced: [brief description]
→ We're working toward: [upcoming topic or skill]

Coming up this week:
→ [Date]: [Event/assignment/test]
→ [Date]: [Event/assignment/test]

What you can do at home:
[1-2 specific suggestions for home reinforcement]

Questions? Email me at [email] or schedule a conference: [link]

[Teacher name]

[PC-02] BEHAVIOR CONCERN EMAIL:
Subject: Check-in about [Student Name]

Hi [Parent Name],

I wanted to reach out because [Student] has been [specific behavior — not "bad"] in class this week. Specifically: [2-3 concrete examples without judgment].

I want to work with you on this, not against anyone. [Student] has real strengths — [specific genuine strength]. I want to support those.

A few questions:
→ Have you noticed anything different at home recently?
→ Is there anything I should know that might be affecting [Student]?

Could we find time for a quick call this week? I'm available [times].

[Teacher name]

[PC-03] ACADEMIC CONCERN EMAIL:
Subject: [Student Name]'s progress in [subject]

Hi [Parent Name],

I wanted to share some observations about [Student]'s progress in [subject]. [Student] is [specific positive]. At the same time, I'm seeing [specific academic challenge — concrete, not vague].

Here's my plan: [what you're doing in class to support]. Here's what could help at home: [specific suggestion].

I'd love to connect to make sure we're aligned. Could we schedule [call/meeting] this week?

[Teacher name]
