LONG DESCRIPTION:

Anxiety and low mood are among the most common human experiences.

Most people manage them alone — without tools, without support, and without knowing that evidence-based techniques exist that genuinely help.

The Mental Health & Anxiety Relief Workbook gives you 300 practical exercises grounded in the same approaches used by therapists (CBT, DBT, ACT, mindfulness) — to use on your own, or alongside professional support.

⚠️ This is an educational resource to complement, not replace, professional mental health care.
