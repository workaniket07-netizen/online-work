POSITIVE DISCIPLINE SCRIPTS (20):
[PD-01] Instead of "Stop crying!" → "I see you're really upset. Come here, let's figure out what's happening."
[PD-02] Instead of "Because I said so" → "I'll explain my reasoning, and I want to hear yours."
[PD-03] Instead of "You're being bad" → "That behavior doesn't work in our family. Here's what we do instead."
[PD-04] Instead of "Calm down!" → "Let's take a breath together. [Model deep breathing] Your feelings make sense — let's talk about them."
[PD-05] Instead of "You never listen" → "I notice when I ask you to [X], it's hard to stop right away. What would help you make that transition?"
[PD-06] For tantrums: "I'm here with you. When you're ready, I'm ready to help." [Don't bargain, don't shame, don't disappear]
[PD-07] For sibling conflict: "Each of you is going to tell me what happened — without interrupting. Then we'll problem-solve together."
[PD-08] For homework refusal: "I understand you're tired. Let's do [X minutes] and then take a break. I'll sit with you."
[PD-09] For lying: "I need you to know that I always want to hear the true version, even if you're worried about getting in trouble. Let's try again."
[PD-10] For disrespect: "That didn't feel respectful. Let's pause and start that again differently. I'll go first: [model the conversation]."

CHORE SYSTEM BY AGE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AGE-APPROPRIATE CHORE CHART

Ages 2-3: Basics with help — put toys in bin, carry own plate to sink, put dirty clothes in hamper

Ages 4-5: Independent basics — set own place at table, wipe spills, put folded laundry in dresser, water one plant, feed pet (with supervision)

Ages 6-8: Real household contribution — make bed daily, unload dishwasher, fold and put away own laundry, sweep floor, take trash out, pack own backpack

Ages 9-12: Building responsibility — cook simple meals, grocery list contribution, vacuum, clean bathroom basics, mow lawn (with supervision), babysit younger siblings briefly

Ages 13+: Full household partnership — plan and cook a dinner, grocery shop with budget, do their own laundry completely, schedule and attend some appointments, contribute to household budgeting

FAMILY MEETING AGENDA:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEEKLY FAMILY MEETING — [Date]

Duration: 20-30 minutes max (kids ages permitting)
Who runs it: Rotate (even young kids can run parts)

AGENDA:
5 min — CELEBRATIONS: Each person shares one win or gratitude from the week
5 min — REVIEW: Did we stick to last week's one commitment? What happened?
5 min — CHALLENGES: What's been hard this week? (No blame — just problem-solving)
10 min — PROBLEM-SOLVING: Choose one family challenge to address together
2 min — NEXT WEEK: Set one family goal + one fun plan for the week
2 min — CLOSING: End with something fun (family handshake, silly phrase, etc.)

RULES:
→ Everyone gets to speak — no interrupting
→ No phones
→ Problems about family systems, not about individuals
→ Decisions by consensus when possible
