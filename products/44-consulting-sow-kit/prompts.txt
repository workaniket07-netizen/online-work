━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MASTER SOW TEMPLATE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STATEMENT OF WORK

PROJECT TITLE: [Name]
CLIENT: [Company name]
CONSULTANT/FREELANCER: [Your name/company]
DATE: [Date]
SOW VERSION: 1.0

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
[2-3 sentence summary of what this engagement is about, the business context, and the desired outcome.]

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES
By the end of this engagement, the client will have:
→ [Measurable outcome 1]
→ [Measurable outcome 2]
→ [Measurable outcome 3]

3. SCOPE OF WORK

IN SCOPE:
→ [Specific deliverable 1]
→ [Specific deliverable 2]
→ [Specific task/activity]
→ [Specific meeting or workshop]

OUT OF SCOPE:
→ [Related thing you are NOT doing]
→ [Ongoing support not included]
→ [Platform/tool/implementation not included]

4. DELIVERABLES
Deliverable | Description | Format | Due Date
[Name] | [What it is] | [PDF/Workshop/Report] | [Date]
[Name] | [What it is] | [Format] | [Date]

5. TIMELINE
Phase 1 — [Name] ([dates]): [What happens]
Phase 2 — [Name] ([dates]): [What happens]
Phase 3 — [Name] ([dates]): [What happens]
Final delivery: [Date]

6. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
Consultant: [What you will do]
Client: [What they must provide — access, decisions, approvals, personnel]

7. ASSUMPTIONS
This SOW assumes:
→ [Client will provide [X] by [date]]
→ [All key stakeholders are available for [X] interviews]
→ [Scope will not expand beyond [X] without a change order]

8. INVESTMENT
Total fee: $[X]
Payment structure: [50% on signing / 50% on delivery] OR [monthly installments]
Expenses: [Included / Client reimburses pre-approved expenses]
Invoicing: [Net 30 / Net 15 / Upfront]

9. CHANGE ORDER PROCESS
Any changes to scope, timeline, or deliverables require a written change order signed by both parties. Change orders will include: description of change, additional cost (if any), timeline impact.

10. TERMS
Confidentiality: [Both parties agree to keep project details confidential]
Intellectual property: [Specify who owns deliverables after payment]
Governing law: [Your state/country]

CONSULTING PROPOSAL TEMPLATE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PROPOSAL: [Project Title]
Prepared for: [Client Name] | Prepared by: [Your Name] | Date: [Date]

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1-2 paragraphs):
[Client] is seeking [objective]. Based on our discovery call and my experience with [relevant background], I propose [brief description of approach]. This engagement will [specific outcome] within [timeframe].

THE SITUATION:
[What's happening for the client — their current state + the opportunity/problem]

THE OPPORTUNITY:
[What success looks like — the specific business outcome they want]

MY APPROACH:
Phase 1: [Name and brief description]
Phase 2: [Name and brief description]
Phase 3: [Name and brief description]

WHAT MAKES ME DIFFERENT:
→ [Differentiator 1 with evidence]
→ [Differentiator 2 with evidence]

RELEVANT EXPERIENCE:
[1-2 relevant case studies or examples — quantified where possible]

INVESTMENT OPTIONS:
Option 1: [Full scope] — $[X]
Option 2: [Reduced scope] — $[X]
Option 3: [Advisory only] — $[X]/month

TIMELINE:
Start date: [Dependent on signed agreement and deposit]
Phase completions: [List]
Final delivery: [Estimated]

NEXT STEPS:
[Single clear action — "Reply with any questions, or sign the attached agreement to proceed."]

This proposal is valid until [date].

CLIENT MANAGEMENT SCRIPTS (40):
[CM-01] Client who won't make decisions: "I need [X decision] by [date] to keep the project on schedule. If I don't hear by then, I'll [proceed with the recommended approach / pause this phase]."
[CM-02] Client who expands scope: "I love the ambition here. This is outside the current SOW — let me scope it as a change order so we can do it properly."
[CM-03] Project going off-track: "I want to flag a risk early: [issue]. Here's my recommendation: [solution]. Can we schedule 30 minutes to align?"
[CM-04 to CM-40] More scripts: budget overrun, timeline slippage, team conflict, feedback incorporation, stakeholder alignment
