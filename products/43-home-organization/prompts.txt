━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE DECLUTTERING DECISION FRAMEWORK:
For every item, ask: "Does this serve my life NOW?" (Not 'might someday')
Keep: Used in last 12 months OR irreplaceable sentimental value
Donate: Good condition, someone else would use it
Recycle: Not in good condition, recyclable material
Trash: Broken, unusable, expired

THE ONE-ROOM DECLUTTER PROCESS:
[DC-01] Set timer: 25 minutes (you'll be surprised how much gets done)
[DC-02] Take everything OUT of the space first
[DC-03] Clean the empty space
[DC-04] Put back ONLY what belongs + what you've decided to keep
[DC-05] Create "donate," "relocate," "trash" piles — remove them SAME DAY

ROOM-BY-ROOM CHECKLISTS (10 rooms):

KITCHEN DECLUTTER CHECKLIST:
□ Expired food — pantry, fridge, freezer
□ Duplicate appliances (do you really need 2 spatulas?)
□ Dishes and glasses you never use
□ Plastic containers without lids (or lids without containers)
□ Expired spices and condiments
□ Junk drawer — sort and reduce
□ Bags of bags (one dedicated bag is enough)
□ Old takeout menus (everything is online now)
□ Broken or chipped items
□ Rarely used appliances taking prime counter/cabinet space

BEDROOM DECLUTTER CHECKLIST:
□ Clothes not worn in 12 months
□ Clothes that don't fit (they're a fantasy, not your reality)
□ Shoes — worn in last year?
□ Books — will you actually re-read them?
□ Bedside table clutter
□ Under-bed storage — is it organized or a black hole?
□ Old medications (check bathroom too)
□ Jewelry you never wear
□ "Just in case" items that have never been needed

BATHROOM DECLUTTER:
□ Expired medications → dispose properly (many pharmacies accept)
□ Expired or unused beauty/skincare products
□ Duplicate toiletries
□ Old towels (donate if still good)
□ Products you bought and didn't like
□ Empty bottles left in shower

[Continue for: living room, kids' rooms, garage, office/desk, dining room, laundry room, entryway]

THE KONMARI SPARK JOY JOURNAL:
[KM-01] Hold each item: Does it spark joy? If not, thank it and let it go.
[KM-02] Category order: Clothes → Books → Papers → Komono (miscellaneous) → Sentimental
[KM-03] Never organize by room — always by category
[KM-04] Sentimental items LAST (you'll have clarity by then)

DAILY CLEANING ROUTINE (15 minutes):
Morning:
□ Make bed immediately (sets the tone for the whole day)
□ Wipe kitchen counter after breakfast
□ Load/unload dishwasher
□ Quick bathroom wipe-down

Evening:
□ Kitchen clean after dinner (5 min)
□ Sweep main floor if needed
□ Clear any surfaces that collected clutter during the day
□ Set out clothes for tomorrow

WEEKLY CLEANING SCHEDULE (zone method):
Monday: Kitchen (deep wipe appliances, mop)
Tuesday: Bathrooms (toilet, sink, shower, mirror)
Wednesday: Dusting throughout + vacuum
Thursday: Floors (all rooms)
Friday: Laundry + bedsheets change
Saturday: Outdoor areas / garage / car
Sunday: Rest + light maintenance only

MONTHLY TASKS:
□ Clean inside oven and microwave
□ Wipe down refrigerator (inside and out)
□ Clean ceiling fans and light fixtures
□ Wash windows (inside)
□ Deep clean bathrooms (grout, behind toilet, under sink)
□ Vacuum mattresses
□ Wipe baseboards
□ Clean inside dishwasher (run empty cycle with cleaner)

SEASONAL TASKS (quarterly):
□ Wash all bedding including comforters
□ Clean inside oven thoroughly
□ HVAC filter change
□ Organize storage areas
□ Deep clean refrigerator including coils
□ Window washing (inside + outside)
□ Declutter seasonal clothing
□ Clean behind and under appliances

SECTION 3: ORGANIZATION SYSTEMS (80 Templates)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PANTRY ORGANIZATION SYSTEM:
[PO-01] Zone by category: Baking | Canned goods | Snacks | Breakfast | Pasta/grains | Spices | Sauces
[PO-02] First in, first out (FIFO): Newer items behind older items
[PO-03] Clear containers for frequently used items (cereal, flour, sugar, nuts)
[PO-04] Labeling system: [Label format: category + contents + date opened]
[PO-05] Weekly pantry check: Before grocery shopping, check pantry to avoid duplicates

CLOSET ORGANIZATION:
[CO-01] The capsule wardrobe approach: [X] items that mix and match for [X] outfits
[CO-02] Sort by category: Tops / Bottoms / Dresses / Outerwear / Formal
[CO-03] Color coding within categories: Visually easier to find items
[CO-04] Seasonal rotation: Off-season to back/high shelf or storage bags
[CO-05] The hanger trick: Turn all hangers backward → after 6 months, whatever's still backward = donate

PAPER & DOCUMENT ORGANIZATION:
[DO-01] Four categories: Action needed | To file | To read | Shred
[DO-02] Physical filing system: Active / Archive / Emergency documents
[DO-03] Emergency document folder: IDs, insurance cards, birth certificates, property documents
[DO-04] Digital backup: Scan and save important documents to secure cloud
[DO-05] Inbox zero for physical mail: Open, sort, act or file SAME DAY

KIDS' ROOM ORGANIZATION:
[KR-01] Toy rotation: Keep 1/3 of toys accessible, rotate monthly — feels like new toys
[KR-02] One in, one out rule: New toy comes in, old toy goes to donate box
[KR-03] Easy access storage: Kids clean up better when bins are at their height + labeled with pictures
[KR-04] Book organization: Books facing out (not spine) at child height → more likely to be chosen

GARAGE ORGANIZATION:
[GO-01] Zones: Car care | Garden tools | Sports equipment | Seasonal items | Tools | Storage
[GO-02] Vertical storage: Wall hooks and shelving make floor space usable
[GO-03] Labeled bins: Clear or with large labels — never mystery boxes
[GO-04] Annual purge: Every spring, one sweep through what hasn't been touched
