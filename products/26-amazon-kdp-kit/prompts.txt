PROFITABLE KDP NICHE FORMULA:
Profitable niche = High demand (BSR under 100,000) + Low competition (under 1,000 reviews on top 3) + Underserved audience (specific sub-niche)

NICHE VALIDATION CHECKLIST:
□ Search keyword on Amazon — are there books in the top 100 with under 150 reviews?
□ Check Best Seller Rank (BSR) of top results — under 100,000 = people are buying
□ Read 1-star reviews of competitors — what do buyers wish was different?
□ Search "[niche] journal" "[niche] workbook" "[niche] planner" — all separate niches
□ Check seasonality — is this niche relevant year-round?

PROVEN HIGH-DEMAND LOW-CONTENT BOOK CATEGORIES:
→ Notebooks/journals (lined, dot grid, blank, cornell)
→ Activity books (word search, coloring, sudoku, crossword)
→ Planners (daily, weekly, monthly, niche-specific)
→ Log books (workout, food, gratitude, habit, reading)
→ Prompt journals (writing prompts, therapy prompts, self-reflection)
→ Kids activity books (mazes, connect the dots, simple coloring)
→ Guest books (wedding, baby shower, vacation rental)
→ Password books (always sells, low competition)
→ Recipe books (blank recipe collection)
→ Budget/finance trackers

BOOK TITLE FORMULAS (30):

[BT-01] THE SPECIFIC OUTCOME TITLE:
"[Timeframe]-Minute [Topic]: [Specific Result] Without [Common Obstacle]"
Example: "5-Minute Gratitude Journal: Build Lasting Happiness Without Feeling Forced"

[BT-02] THE AUDIENCE-FIRST TITLE:
"The [Audience] [Book Type]: [Benefit]"
Example: "The Anxious Mom's Gratitude Journal: Daily Prompts for Peace of Mind"

[BT-03] THE HOW-TO TITLE:
"How to [Goal] Using [Method]: [Subtitle with more detail]"
Example: "How to Manifest Anything: A 90-Day Journal for Attracting Your Ideal Life"

[BT-04] THE YEAR TITLE:
"[Year] [Book Type] for [Audience]: [Benefit]"
Example: "2025 Daily Planner for Entrepreneurs: More Focus, Less Overwhelm"

[BT-05] THE NUMBER TITLE:
"[Number] [Things]: A [Book Type] for [Audience]"
Example: "365 Journal Prompts: A Year of Deep Self-Discovery"

[BT-06] THE COMPLETE/ULTIMATE TITLE:
"The Complete [Topic] [Book Type]: [What's Included]"
Example: "The Complete Keto Food Journal: Track Macros, Meals, and Progress"

"Guided [Book Type]: [Journey/Transformation]"

KDP BOOK LAUNCH CHECKLIST:
Pre-launch (2 weeks before):
□ Cover finalized (professional design — this is 80% of success)
□ Description written and optimized
□ 7 keywords researched and entered
□ 2 categories chosen (one easy = faster bestseller badge)
□ Pricing set ($127.99 for paperback launch or free Kindle for reviews)
□ Author Central profile created
□ ARC (Advance Review Copy) sent to 10 readers

Launch week:
□ Price at $127.99 for first 3 days → normal price after
□ Share to social media (Pinterest best for journals)
□ Email list announcement
□ Ask 5 friends/family to buy + review on day 1
□ Amazon Ads campaign started (optional)
