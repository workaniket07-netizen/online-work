SECTION 5: CREATOR BUSINESS MODEL GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE 6 INCOME STREAMS FOR CREATORS — AND WHEN TO ADD EACH:

STREAM 1: AD REVENUE (YouTube/Podcasts)
When to start: YouTube 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours | Podcast: when podcast network offers deal
Revenue: $127-8 CPM (YouTube), variable (podcast)
Best for: Long-form video, educational, news content
Not ideal for: Short-form only, niche B2B audiences

STREAM 2: BRAND DEALS / SPONSORSHIPS
When to start: Any size if you have an engaged, relevant audience (micro-influencers with 5k+ convert well)
Revenue: $127-200 CPM typically. Rates vary wildly by niche.
Best niche for brand deals: Finance, tech, fitness, beauty, business, education
Key: Audience trust is more valuable than audience size

STREAM 3: DIGITAL PRODUCTS
When to start: Once you know what your audience needs (surveys + comment analysis)
Revenue: 80-90% margin. Most scalable income stream.
Best products: Templates, guides, courses, swipe files, databases, toolkits
Price range: $127-$997 depending on depth and audience budget

STREAM 4: PAID COMMUNITY / MEMBERSHIP
When to start: When free audience regularly asks for more access/connection
Revenue: $127-197/month per member. Predictable, recurring.
Best for: Accountability-based niches (business, fitness, parenting), highly specialized knowledge

STREAM 5: SERVICES / CONSULTING
When to start: Immediately (you don't need an audience — but having one helps)
Revenue: $500-50,000+ per engagement
Best for: High-skill creators with specialized knowledge
Caveat: Not scalable on its own. Use to fund product development.

STREAM 6: AFFILIATE MARKETING
When to start: Once you've used and genuinely endorse a product
Revenue: 10-50% commission on referred sales
Best for: Tools, SaaS, courses. Works in any niche.
Key: Only promote products you've used. Audience trust is your business.

INCOME STREAM LAUNCH ORDER (recommended):
1. Services/consulting (immediate cash)
2. Digital products (scalable, passive)
3. Brand deals (when audience exists)
4. Affiliate marketing (add to existing content)
5. Paid community (when demand is clear)
6. Ad revenue (passive, low priority at first)
