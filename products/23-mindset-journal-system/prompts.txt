MANIFESTATION JOURNAL METHOD:
Write as if it has already happened. Present tense. Specific detail. Emotion.
This is not magical thinking — it's directed attention, identity rehearsal, and clarity about what you actually want.

VISUALIZATION GUIDES (20)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[VG-01] THE 5-MINUTE MORNING VISUALIZATION:
"Close your eyes. Take 3 deep breaths.
See yourself 12 months from now living your ideal day.
Where are you? What do you see, hear, feel?
Who is with you?
What have you accomplished that you haven't yet?
How do you feel waking up to this life?
Stay in this scene for 2-3 minutes.
When you open your eyes, write: what was most vivid? What emotions came up?"

[VG-02] THE GOAL ACHIEVEMENT VISUALIZATION:
"Imagine your [specific goal] is complete. You've done it.
Close your eyes and picture: the moment you knew it happened, where you were, who you told first, how your body felt, what changed about how you walk into a room.
Write it all down. This is your evidence that it's possible."

SECTION 4: LIMITING BELIEF REFRAMES (60 Prompts)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The most common limiting beliefs — with journaling prompts to examine and release each.

[LB-01] "I'm not good enough"
Examine: Where did this belief come from? Who told you this — directly or indirectly?
Evidence: Name 5 times you were clearly good enough.
Reframe: What would a loving mentor say about this belief?
New belief: "I am [specific quality]."

[LB-02] "I don't deserve [love/success/good things]"
Examine: What would have to be true about the world for this to be a fact?
Evidence: Name 3 ways you've shown up in ways that are worthy of love/success.
Reframe: Deserving is not something you earn. It's something you decide.
New belief: "I receive [thing] with ease because [reason]."

[LB-03] "I always [negative pattern]"
Examine: "Always" is never true. Name 3 times the pattern didn't happen.
Examine: What function does this pattern serve? What is it protecting you from?
Reframe: "Sometimes I [pattern] when I feel [trigger]. I can choose differently."

[LB-04] "It's too late for me"
Examine: Too late according to whose timeline?
Evidence: Name someone who started [goal] after your age and succeeded.
Reframe: "I'm starting exactly when I was meant to start. What I gain from delay: [experience/perspective/clarity]."

[LB-05] "I'm not [smart/talented/creative/experienced] enough"
The comparison trap: You're comparing your internal experience to others' external performance.
Evidence: Beginners in [area] you admire were once exactly where you are.
Reframe: "I have exactly [what I have]. It's enough to start."

[LB-06 to LB-30] More limiting beliefs:
Fear of failure | Fear of success | Fear of visibility | Imposter syndrome | "I'm too much/too sensitive" | "I can't trust people" | "Money is hard to make" | "People like me don't [succeed]" | "I'm not consistent enough" | "I'll be happy when [future thing]" | "My past defines me"

[LB-31 to LB-60] Reframe worksheet format for any belief:
State the belief → Examine origin → Find contradictory evidence → Question the usefulness → Write new belief → Repeat daily for 21 days
