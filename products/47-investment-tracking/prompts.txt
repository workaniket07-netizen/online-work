VALUE INVESTING CHECKLIST (buffett-inspired):
[VI-01] Is the business simple and understandable?
[VI-02] Does it have a consistent operating history (10+ years)?
[VI-03] Does it have favorable long-term prospects (durable competitive advantage)?
[VI-04] Is management rational and shareholder-friendly?
[VI-05] Is the business return on equity consistently high (>15%)?
[VI-06] What are owner earnings (net income + depreciation - capex)?
[VI-07] Is it available at a significant discount to intrinsic value?

FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS QUICK SCREEN:
[FA-01] Revenue growth: 3-year avg > 10% = strong
[FA-02] Earnings growth: 3-year avg > 10% = strong
[FA-03] Gross margin: > industry average = competitive advantage
[FA-04] Operating margin: Expanding over time = pricing power
[FA-05] Free cash flow: Positive and growing = real profits
[FA-06] Debt/EBITDA: < 3x = manageable debt
[FA-07] Return on invested capital (ROIC): > 10% = efficient use of capital
[FA-08] P/E vs growth (PEG): < 1 = potentially undervalued

ETF/INDEX FUND SELECTION FRAMEWORK:
[EF-01] Expense ratio: Lower is better. Target < 0.20%
[EF-02] AUM: Larger fund = more liquid = smaller bid/ask spread
[EF-03] Tracking error: How closely does it track its index?
[EF-04] Index methodology: Market cap weighted / equal weighted / factor weighted
[EF-05] Tax efficiency: ETFs generally more tax-efficient than mutual funds

MARKET CRASH PROTOCOL:
When market drops 20%+:
Step 1: Re-read my Investment Policy Statement
Step 2: Ask: Has my investment thesis changed? (Usually no)
Step 3: If thesis intact: continue DCA, possibly increase contributions
Step 4: DO NOT sell based on fear
Step 5: Do not check portfolio more than weekly

"Time in market > timing the market."

COMMON INVESTOR MISTAKES CHECKLIST:
[MI-01] Selling winners too early, holding losers too long (disposition effect)
[MI-02] Overconfidence in individual stock picking
[MI-03] Recency bias (assuming recent performance continues)
[MI-04] Not diversifying across sectors/geographies
[MI-05] Timing the market instead of time in market
[MI-06] Ignoring taxes (harvesting losses, avoiding short-term gains)
[MI-07] Chasing yield (high dividend doesn't mean safe investment)
[MI-08] Neglecting to rebalance (drift creates unintended risk)
