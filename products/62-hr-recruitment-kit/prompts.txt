━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

JOB DESCRIPTION FORMULA:
[JD-01] Section 1: Company/team intro (2-3 sentences — culture and mission, not just description)
[JD-02] Section 2: The role in 2 sentences (what they'll own, who they'll impact)
[JD-03] Section 3: What you'll do (5-8 bullets, outcomes not tasks)
[JD-04] Section 4: What we're looking for (must-have vs nice-to-have — separate clearly)
[JD-05] Section 5: What we offer (compensation range, benefits, growth, culture)
[JD-06] Section 6: Application process (what to expect, timeline)

BIAS-FREE LANGUAGE GUIDE:
Replace → With:
"Rockstar/Ninja/Guru" → "Experienced [Role]"
"Must have X years" → "Proven experience in X"
"Strong communicator" → Describe specific communication needed
"Culture fit" → "Aligned with our values of [specific values]"
"Must be available nights/weekends" → Specify actual schedule requirements

FULL RECRUITING CYCLE TEMPLATES:

SOURCING:
[RS-01] LinkedIn outreach (cold): "Hi [Name], I came across your profile while looking for [role] candidates. Your background in [specific detail] caught my attention. [Company] is hiring a [Role] — would you be open to a quick conversation?"
[RS-02] LinkedIn outreach (warm): "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out. We're hiring for [role] and your background seems like a great fit."
[RS-03] Referral request to employees: "We're hiring [Role]! Know anyone? Here's the JD: [link]. We offer [referral bonus]."
[RS-04 to RS-10] More sourcing templates

SCREENING:
[SC-01] Phone screen questions (10 per role type)
[SC-02] Application screening rubric
[SC-03] Skills assessment cover email
[SC-04] Video interview instructions

INTERVIEWING:
[IV-01] Interview confirmation email (what to bring, who they'll meet, timing)
[IV-02] Interview guide per competency (behavioral + situational questions)
[IV-03] Scoring rubric template (1-5 scale, standardized across interviewers)
[IV-04] Debrief agenda template

OFFERS:

[OF-03] Offer negotiation responses (3 scenarios)

REJECTIONS:

[RJ-02] After phone screen: "We appreciated our conversation..."

[RJ-04] Feedback policy (when/how to give interview feedback)
