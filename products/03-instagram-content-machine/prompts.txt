SECTION 5: BONUS — INSTAGRAM GROWTH STRATEGY GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE 3-CONTENT-TYPE SYSTEM (for consistent growth):

1. REACH POSTS (30% of content): Get discovered by new people
   → Reels with trending audio
   → Educational content with broad appeal
   → Controversial or relatable posts that get shared
   Goal: Impressions, reach, new followers

2. NURTURE POSTS (50% of content): Build trust with existing audience
   → Behind the scenes
   → Personal stories
   → Value-heavy tutorials
   → Community engagement
   Goal: Saves, comments, DMs

3. CONVERT POSTS (20% of content): Turn followers to buyers
   → Product features
   → Testimonials
   → Offers and CTAs
   Goal: Clicks, link visits, sales

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE IDEAL WEEKLY POSTING SCHEDULE:

Monday: Educational carousel (NURTURE)
Tuesday: Reel — trending or tutorial (REACH)
Wednesday: Product/service feature (CONVERT)
Thursday: Story — Q&A or personal (NURTURE)
Friday: Reel — relatable or entertainment (REACH)
Saturday: Community post or appreciation (NURTURE)
Sunday: Week prep content + teaser for next week (REACH + NURTURE)

Stories: 3-5 per day, mix of polls, behind scenes, tips

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE 5-3-2 ENGAGEMENT RULE:
For every 10 posts you want, engage with 5 posts in your niche, 3 posts from similar accounts, 2 posts from your ideal followers.
Engagement drives Instagram to show YOUR content more.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HASHTAG STRATEGY 2025-2026:
• Use 5-10 hashtags max (not 30)
• Mix: 2 mega (1M+), 3 large (100k-1M), 3 niche (10k-100k), 2 micro (1k-10k)
• Create 1 brand hashtag: #[BRANDNAME]
• Rotate 3-4 sets of hashtags to avoid shadow-ban
• Put hashtags in first comment, not caption

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

REELS BEST PRACTICES:
• Hook must appear in first 1-2 seconds
• Add text overlay — 85% watch without sound
• Use trending audio (look for upward arrow on songs)
• Keep under 60 seconds for best completion rate
• End with a question or CTA to trigger comments
• Post between 8-10am or 7-9pm in your audience's timezone
