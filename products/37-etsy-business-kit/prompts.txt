ETSY LISTING SEO SYSTEM:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TITLE FORMULA (140 characters max):
[Primary keyword] | [Secondary keyword] | [Attribute] for [Occasion/Use Case] | [Style/Material]
Example: "Minimalist Wedding Invitation Template | Editable Canva | Simple Elegant | Printable Digital Download"

TITLE RULES:
→ Put most important keyword FIRST (Etsy weights front-loading)
→ Include: what it is + who it's for + how it's used
→ Avoid: articles (a/an/the save space), trademarked terms

TAG STRATEGY (13 tags maximum):
→ Tags 1-3: Long-tail phrases (3-4 words) with highest buyer intent
→ Tags 4-8: Secondary phrases covering different search angles
→ Tags 9-11: Style/material/occasion descriptors
→ Tags 12-13: Broader category terms
→ Never repeat words between title and tags — new search coverage

KEYWORD RESEARCH METHOD:
1. Type seed keyword in Etsy search bar
2. Note all autocomplete suggestions = real searches
3. Use eRank or Marmalead for volume data
4. Target: medium-volume, medium-competition = fastest growth

[CS-02] "My order never arrived (physical)": "I'm so sorry to hear that! Let me check the tracking right away. [Check tracking]. [If delivered]: The carrier shows delivered on [date]. Sometimes packages end up with neighbors or in an unexpected spot. Could you check those? [If lost]: I'll file a claim and get you a replacement right away."
[CS-03] "Item is damaged": "I'm so sorry your order arrived damaged — that's not acceptable. Please send me a photo of the damage and I'll [replace it immediately / issue a refund] right away. No need to return the damaged item."
[CS-04] "I want a refund": "I understand. [Your policy]: [digital: due to instant access, I can't offer refunds on digital downloads unless the file is corrupted] / [physical: happy to refund or exchange within X days of delivery]. What would work best for you?"
