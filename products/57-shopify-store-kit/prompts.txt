PRODUCT RESEARCH FRAMEWORK:
[PR-01] Winning product criteria: solves a clear problem + not mass market + $127-80 price + light/small + 3x margin possible
[PR-02] Trend validation: Google Trends + TikTok search + Pinterest + Amazon Best Sellers
[PR-03] Competition check: Under 50 reviews on top Shopify stores = opportunity
[PR-04] Profit calculator: (Selling price - Product cost - Shipping - Platform fees - Ads) / Selling price = profit margin. Target: 30%+

PRODUCT PAGE COPY TEMPLATES:
[PP-01] HERO SECTION:
Headline: [Main Benefit] — [Secondary Benefit]
Subheadline: [Who it's for + specific outcome]
Primary CTA: "Add to Cart — [ships in X days]"
Trust badge: "[X] happy customers | Free returns | Secure checkout"

[PP-02] PRODUCT DESCRIPTION (formula):
Pain point (first sentence) → Solution (your product) → How it works (3 bullets) → Why it's different (1-2 sentences) → Social proof → Features (scannable) → Guarantee → CTA

[PP-03] PRODUCT BULLETS (benefit-first format):
"[Benefit] — [Feature that enables it]: [Quick proof or detail]"
Example: "Never run out of battery — 6,000mAh power bank: Charges your phone 2.5x from 0"

THE 5 ESSENTIAL SHOPIFY EMAIL FLOWS:

[EF-01] WELCOME SERIES (5 emails, 7 days):
Email 1 (Immediate): Welcome + brand story + bestsellers
Email 2 (Day 2): Educational content related to your niche
Email 3 (Day 4): Social proof + testimonials + FAQ
Email 4 (Day 6): Discount offer (10% off first order, 48hr limit)
Email 5 (Day 7): Last chance + what makes you different

[EF-02] ABANDONED CART (3 emails):
Email 1 (1 hour): "You left something behind" + what they added + CTA
Email 2 (24 hours): "Still thinking?" + add social proof + answer objection
Email 3 (72 hours): "Last chance" + small discount or free shipping offer

[EF-03] POST-PURCHASE SEQUENCE (4 emails):
Email 1: Order confirmation + expected delivery + what to expect
Email 2: Shipping confirmation + tracking
Email 3 (day after delivery): How to use/enjoy + care tips
Email 4 (7 days later): Review request + loyalty offer

[EF-04] WIN-BACK (3 emails, 90+ day inactive):
Email 1: "Miss you" + what's new
Email 2: "Something you might like" + personalized product
Email 3: "Final offer" + discount + we might remove you

[EF-05] VIP/LOYALTY PROGRAM:
Tiered system: Bronze/Silver/Gold based on spend
Email templates for each tier milestone

[EF-06 to EF-80] More email templates:
Sale announcement, product launch, seasonal campaigns, referral program, subscription upsell, product education series

□ Product images: 5+ photos, lifestyle, zoom, 360° if possible
□ Price anchoring: Show original price + sale price
□ Scarcity: "Only [X] left" or "Selling fast"
□ Social proof: Reviews above the fold, average rating visible
□ Trust badges: Secure payment, free returns, guarantee
□ Mobile optimization: 70%+ traffic is mobile
□ Page speed: Under 3 seconds load time
□ Clear CTA: "Add to Cart" button stands out
□ Upsell offers: "Frequently bought with" section
□ Cross-sell: "Complete the look" or "Bundle and save"
□ Free shipping threshold: "Add $X more for free shipping"
