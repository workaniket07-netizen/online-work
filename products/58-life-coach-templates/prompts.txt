90 POWERFUL COACHING QUESTIONS (by topic):
IDENTITY QUESTIONS:
[CQ-01] "Who would you be if you weren't afraid?"
[CQ-02] "What parts of yourself have you been hiding?"
[CQ-03] "When do you feel most alive and like yourself?"
[CQ-04] "What does the best version of you look like in 5 years?"
[CQ-05] "What story do you keep telling yourself that might not be true?"

GOAL + VISION QUESTIONS:
[CQ-06] "If you knew you couldn't fail, what would you attempt?"
[CQ-07] "What would your life look like if [the problem] was completely solved?"
[CQ-08] "What's one thing you've been putting off that, if done, would change everything?"
[CQ-09] "What does success actually mean to you — not society, not your parents — YOU?"
[CQ-10] "What does your ideal ordinary Tuesday look like in 3 years?"

BELIEF CHANGE QUESTIONS:
[CQ-11] "Where did you first learn to believe [limiting belief]?"
[CQ-12] "Who taught you that you weren't [worthy/capable/enough]?"
[CQ-13] "What would you need to believe about yourself to [achieve goal]?"
[CQ-14] "What's the cost of holding onto this belief?"
[CQ-15] "Can you find 3 pieces of evidence that this belief is wrong?"

[CQ-16 to CQ-90] More questions: relationship, career, habits, health, money, creativity

DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT:
[DC-01] Opening (2 min): "Tell me what brought you here today."
[DC-02] Deep dive (20 min): "Say more about that. How long has this been going on? What have you tried? What would change if this was solved?"
[DC-03] Vision (10 min): "What would your life look like if we solved this completely?"
[DC-04] Gap + stakes (5 min): "What happens if nothing changes?"
[DC-05] Presenting the offer (10 min): "Based on what you've shared, here's what working together looks like..."
[DC-06] Handling objections (varies)
[DC-07] Close (3 min): "Does this feel like the right next step for you?"

OBJECTION HANDLING (15):
[OB-01] "It's expensive": "Compared to what? What's the cost of staying exactly where you are for another year?"
[OB-02] "I need to think about it": "What specifically is making you hesitate? Let's talk about it now."
[OB-03] "I need to ask my partner": "Of course. What would help you have that conversation?"
[OB-04] "I'm not sure I'm ready": "What would ready look like for you?"
[OB-05 to OB-15] More objection responses
