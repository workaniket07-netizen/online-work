CHANNEL NICHE VALIDATION:
[NV-01] Search volume test: Your niche keyword in YouTube search — does autocomplete show variations? = demand
[NV-02] Competition test: Top 10 results — any channels under 100k subscribers getting 50k+ views? = room to grow
[NV-03] Monetization test: Are there ads on competitor videos? = advertisers paying = you can earn
[NV-04] Evergreen test: Will this topic matter in 3 years? = longevity

CHANNEL DESCRIPTION TEMPLATES (5):
[CD-01] Niche + audience + value: "Welcome to [Channel Name] — where [target audience] come to [specific benefit]. I post [content type] every [frequency] to help you [outcome]."
[CD-02] Mission-first: "On this channel, I believe [core belief about your topic]. That's why I create [content type] that [specific value]. New videos every [day]."
[CD-03 to CD-05] More: personality-led, problem-solver, community-focused

ABOUT PAGE OPTIMIZATION:
→ Include top 3 keywords in first 100 characters (shows in search)
→ List what you post + when
→ Link to website/other platforms
→ Include email for business inquiries

CHANNEL KEYWORD RESEARCH:
[KW-01] Tools: TubeBuddy / VidIQ (free tiers) — install to see video tag suggestions
[KW-02] Method: Search your topic → note autocomplete → check "suggested keywords" in TubeBuddy → find medium competition/high volume
[KW-03] Tag strategy: Exact title keywords → related keywords → broad category keywords

THUMBNAIL PRINCIPLES:
[TH-01] The 3-element rule: Face emotion + Bold text (max 4 words) + Contrasting color
[TH-02] High contrast: Dark background + light text OR light background + dark text
[TH-03] Face-forward: Videos with expressive faces get 30%+ more CTR
[TH-04] Consistency: Same font/style across thumbnails = channel recognition
[TH-05] Test: Would you click this thumbnail if someone else made it?

TAGS STRATEGY:
[TS-01] First tag = exact video title phrase
[TS-02] Tags 2-5: Variations of main keyword
[TS-03] Tags 6-10: Related topics viewer might search
[TS-04] Tags 11-15: Broad category terms
[TS-05] Total: 10-15 tags, 300-500 characters max

[CM-04] Community post formats: Polls / Behind scenes / Sneak peak / Question / Celebration milestone

COLLABORATIONS OUTREACH (20):
