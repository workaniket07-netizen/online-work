THE 0 TO 10K FOLLOWER ROADMAP:
Week 1-2: Foundation
→ Optimize bio: clear niche + specific audience + proof point
→ Pin a high-value thread
→ Post 3x/day: 1 standalone tweet + 1 reply to big account + 1 original thread
→ Comment on 10 accounts in your niche daily

Month 1: Momentum
→ Find 5 accounts with 10k-50k followers in your niche
→ Reply to every tweet they post within 30 minutes (quality replies)
→ Post one thread per week
→ Engage with every mention/reply within 1 hour

Month 2-3: Acceleration
→ Start posting threads 3x/week
→ Identify your best-performing tweet format and double it
→ Guest posts / collabs with similar-sized accounts
→ Begin building email list from followers
