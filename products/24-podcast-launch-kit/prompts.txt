SECTION 1: PODCAST LAUNCH SYSTEM (60 Templates)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRE-LAUNCH CHECKLIST:
□ Define your niche (specific enough to be findable, broad enough to sustain)
□ Choose your format (solo / interview / co-host / narrative)
□ Choose episode length (15-20 min = most completed, 45-60 min = most loyal)
□ Name your podcast (include keyword for search)
□ Create cover art (3000×3000 px, eye-catching, text readable at small size)
□ Write podcast description (what it's about, who it's for, why listen)
□ Choose hosting platform (Buzzsprout / Anchor / Podbean / Transistor)
□ Record 3 episodes before launch (launch with 3 to boost rankings)
□ Set up RSS feed and submit to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Google Podcasts
□ Create trailer episode (60-90 seconds: who you are + what show is + why listen)
□ Set up podcast website or page
□ Create social media account for the podcast
□ Plan first 10 episode topics

Show name: [name that includes your keyword]

Episode format: [solo / interview / both]

TRAILER SCRIPT TEMPLATE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Hi — I'm [Your Name], and this is [Podcast Name].

[Podcast Name] is for [specific audience] who want to [specific outcome].

Every [week/episode/time], I [what you do — interview experts / share strategies / tell stories] about [topic].

If you've ever [relatable challenge your listener faces], you're in the right place.

I started this show because [genuine personal reason — 1-2 sentences].

Subscribe wherever you listen to podcasts — new episodes drop every [day/frequency].

Let's get into it."

EPISODE INTRO TEMPLATES (5 styles):

[EI-01] STANDARD INTRO:
"Welcome back to [Show Name] — I'm your host, [Name]. Today's episode is about [topic], and here's why it matters for you: [specific benefit/problem it solves]. Let's dive in."

[EI-02] STORY HOOK INTRO:
"[Open with a story that illustrates the episode's main point — 3-4 sentences]. That story is why today's episode is about [topic]. By the end of this conversation, you'll know [specific takeaway]. I'm [Name], this is [Show Name], and here's [guest name / today's topic]."

[EI-03] QUESTION HOOK INTRO:
"Have you ever [relatable question directly to listener]? If you have, you're not alone — and this episode was made for you. I'm [Name] on [Show Name], and today we're covering [topic] with [guest if applicable]. Stay with me — [teaser of best insight from episode]."

[EI-04] STAT/FACT HOOK INTRO:
"[Surprising statistic or fact about your topic]. I'm [Name], and on today's episode of [Show Name], we're exploring [topic] — why [stat context], what to do about it, and [specific outcome listener gets]. Let's go."

[EI-05] RETURNING LISTENERS INTRO:
"For those who are new — welcome, grab a drink, and hit subscribe. For my regular listeners — you asked me about [topic] more than anything else, so I built this whole episode around it. [Topic], [subtopic], [specific insight coming]. Let's get into it."

EPISODE OUTRO TEMPLATES (5 styles):

[EO-01] STANDARD OUTRO:
"That's it for today's episode of [Show Name]. Thank you so much for listening. If this was valuable, the best thing you can do is share it with someone who needs to hear it. I'll be back [next week / on [day]] with [teaser of next episode]. Until then — [your sign-off phrase]."

[EO-02] CTA OUTRO:
"Thank you for spending [X minutes] with me today. Before you go: [specific CTA — subscribe, leave a review, visit website, join community]. You can find everything at [website]. I'll see you next [day] — take care."

[EO-03] REVIEW REQUEST OUTRO:
"One small ask: if you got value from this episode, leave a review on Apple Podcasts. It takes 30 seconds and it helps this show reach more people who need it. I read every single one. And I'll see you next episode."

[EO-04] COMMUNITY OUTRO:
"I'd love to keep this conversation going — come find me on [platform] at [handle]. Tell me: what was your biggest takeaway from today? I reply to everyone. See you there, and I'll be back [next week]."

[EO-05] SERIES OUTRO:
"This is part [X] of the [series name] series. If you haven't heard the previous episodes, I'll link them in the show notes. And next [week/episode], we're covering [topic] — subscribe so you don't miss it."

INTERVIEW QUESTION FRAMEWORKS (30 questions):

OPENING QUESTIONS (use 1-2):
[IQ-01] "For people who don't know you yet — what's your quick origin story? How did you get into [field]?"
[IQ-02] "What are you most focused on right now in your work?"
[IQ-03] "I want to start with a moment — tell me about [specific thing from their background that connects to episode topic]."

DEEP DIVE QUESTIONS (use 5-8):
[IQ-04] "Walk me through [specific thing they did/built/experienced] — what actually happened?"
[IQ-05] "What's the thing most people misunderstand about [topic]?"
[IQ-06] "What's the question nobody asks you that you wish they would?"
[IQ-07] "What changed your mind about [topic/approach]?"
[IQ-08] "Tell me about a failure that turned into something important."
[IQ-09] "What do you know now that you didn't know when you started [thing]?"
[IQ-10] "Who influenced how you think about [topic]?"
[IQ-11] "What does [topic] look like when done really well vs. average?"
[IQ-12] "What's a counterintuitive thing you believe about [field]?"
[IQ-13] "Give me the version of [topic] that you tell close friends vs. what you say publicly."

ACTIONABLE QUESTIONS (use 2-3):
[IQ-14] "For someone just starting with [topic] — what's the first thing to focus on?"
[IQ-15] "What's one thing someone could do this week that would make the biggest difference?"
[IQ-16] "What tool, book, or habit has changed how you work?"

SIGNATURE CLOSING QUESTION (pick one — ask every guest):
[IQ-17] "What's one thing you believe that most people in your field would disagree with?"
[IQ-18] "If you could leave our listeners with one thing — what would it be?"
[IQ-19] "What's the question no one ever asks you that has the most important answer?"
[IQ-20] "What's something you've changed your mind on recently — and why?"
