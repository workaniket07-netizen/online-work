Hi [Guest Name]!

Here's what you need to know:

📍 ADDRESS: [Address] — [brief landmark note if helpful]

See you on [date]!
[Your name]

MESSAGE 2: PRE-ARRIVAL REMINDER (Send: 3 days before check-in)

Hi [Name]! Your stay at [Property] is just [X days] away — getting excited for you!

Here's everything for a smooth arrival:

🔑 ACCESS CODE: [Code for lock/gate/parking]
📶 WIFI: Network: [Name] | Password: [Password]
🧰 HOUSE MANUAL: [Link to digital guidebook or "inside the property"]

⚠️ PLEASE NOTE:
→ [Any important reminder: shoes off, quiet hours, parking rules, etc.]
→ [Where to find supplies — towels, extra blankets, coffee]

Emergency contact during your stay: [Your name + number]

Have an amazing trip — can't wait to hear how it goes!

---

MESSAGE 3: CHECK-IN DAY (Send: Morning of check-in)

Good morning [Name]! Today's the day! 🎉

Quick reminder: check-in starts at [time]. If you're running early or late, just send me a message.

Your door code: [Code]
Parking: [Spot/instruction]
WiFi: [Network + password if not sent yet]

One tip from me: [Local insider tip — best coffee shop nearby, best time to visit main attraction, hidden gem]

I'm available at [number/Airbnb message] if you need anything. Enjoy every moment!

---

MESSAGE 4: MID-STAY CHECK-IN (Send: Day 2 or halfway through)

Hi [Name], just checking in to make sure everything is comfortable!

Is the temperature okay? Anything you need that I can arrange?

If anything feels off or you can't find something, please message me — I'd much rather fix something now than hear about it in the review.

Hope you're having an amazing time. 😊

---

MESSAGE 5: PRE-CHECK-OUT (Send: Night before departure)

Hi [Name], hope your stay has been wonderful!

Just a quick note — checkout is tomorrow at [time].

Before you leave:
□ [Specific checkout instruction 1 — e.g., "Start the dishwasher if used"]
□ [Instruction 2 — e.g., "Leave keys on counter / lock door from inside"]
□ [Instruction 3 — e.g., "Turn off lights and AC/heat"]
□ No need to strip the bed — I take care of that!

Thank you so much for staying. If you've had a great time, a review would mean the world to me — I'll leave one for you too!

Safe travels home, [Name].

---

MESSAGE 6: POST-CHECKOUT (Send: 1-2 hours after checkout)

Hi [Name]! Hope you made it home safely.

It was a pleasure having you as a guest! You were wonderful and left the space perfectly.

I've already left you a glowing review. If you have a moment to share your experience, it makes an enormous difference for a small host like me.

Hoping to welcome you back someday! 😊

[Your Name]
