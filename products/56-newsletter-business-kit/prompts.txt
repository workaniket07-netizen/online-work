THE 5 NEWSLETTER FORMATS THAT BUILD LOYAL READERS:

FORMAT 1: THE INSIGHT + ACTION (most popular)
[NF-01] Section 1: One big insight from this week [400 words]
[NF-02] Section 2: What I'd do about it [200 words]
[NF-03] Section 3: 3 quick links worth reading [bullets]
[NF-04] Section 4: Question of the week [engagement]

FORMAT 2: THE CURATION + COMMENTARY
[NF-05] 5-7 best links from the week + 2-3 sentence take on each
[NF-06] Why: Easy to produce, high value, consistent

FORMAT 3: THE LONG FORM ESSAY
[NF-07] One topic, deep dive, 800-1500 words
[NF-08] Why: Builds authority, shareable, SEO if web version

FORMAT 4: THE INTERVIEW FORMAT
[NF-09] Q&A with someone interesting in your niche
[NF-10] Why: Low writing effort, builds network, audience value

FORMAT 5: THE DATA/RESEARCH BREAKDOWN
[NF-11] You find one interesting study/data point
[NF-12] Break it down for your audience with implications
[NF-13] Why: High shares, establishes expertise
