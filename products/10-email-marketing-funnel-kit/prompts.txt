Open rates: 50-80%. Use it to set expectations, deliver value, and prime for purchase.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5-EMAIL WELCOME SEQUENCE (DIGITAL PRODUCT)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[WS-01] EMAIL 1: THE IMMEDIATE WELCOME (Send: Immediately)

SUBJECT: You're in — here's your [free resource/download/access]
PREVIEW TEXT: Plus, what to expect from me going forward.

Hi [First Name],

Welcome to [Your Community/List Name] — I'm glad you're here.

As promised, here's your [free resource]: [Link/attachment]

[1-2 sentences on what it is and the fastest way to use it]

Over the next few days, I'm going to share [brief overview of sequence]:
→ [Email 2 topic — a key insight or lesson]
→ [Email 3 topic — a resource or strategy]
→ [Email 4 topic — a relevant story or case study]
→ [Email 5 topic — your best offer or invitation]

One thing to know about me:
[2-3 sentences introducing yourself — who you are, what you've done, why you're qualified on this topic]

Talk soon,
[Your Name]

P.S. Hit reply and tell me: [one question about their situation]. I read every reply.

---

[WS-02] EMAIL 2: THE VALUE EMAIL (Send: Day 2)

SUBJECT: The [topic] mistake most [audience] make
PREVIEW TEXT: And a better way to think about it.

Hi [First Name],

Quick one today.

The biggest mistake I see [target audience] make with [topic]:

[Describe the mistake clearly — 2-3 sentences]

Here's why it happens: [Root cause — 2-3 sentences]

The better approach: [Your method/insight — 4-6 sentences with specificity]

[Optional: Example or proof point]

Save this — you'll want it when [relevant moment].

[Name]

P.S. [Optional: tease what's coming in Email 3]

---

[WS-03] EMAIL 3: THE STORY EMAIL (Send: Day 3-4)

SUBJECT: When I [relevant low point or challenge]
PREVIEW TEXT: What happened next changed everything.

Hi [First Name],

[Time period], I was [relatable struggle your audience has].

[3-4 sentences telling the honest story — the challenge, the low point, what you tried]

Then [turning point].

[What changed — 3-4 sentences on the insight or action that shifted things]

Result: [Specific outcome]

Why I'm telling you this: [The lesson you want them to take away — 2 sentences]

[Name]

[LS-01] PRE-LAUNCH EMAIL 1 — THE PROBLEM AGITATOR (7 days before)

SUBJECT: The [topic] problem nobody's talking about
PREVIEW TEXT: This is why [bad outcome] keeps happening.

Hi [First Name],

Something I've been thinking about a lot lately:

[State the problem your product solves — 3-4 sentences. Paint a clear picture of the pain]

Most [target audience] deal with this by [common wrong solution] — and it makes things worse.

[Why common solutions fail — 2-3 sentences]

Over the next week, I'm going to share [what you'll be sending — tease the launch].

Watch for my email on [date] — I think it'll change how you think about [topic].

[Name]

---

[LS-02] PRE-LAUNCH EMAIL 2 — THE BELIEF SHIFTER (5 days before)

SUBJECT: What [result] actually requires
PREVIEW TEXT: It's not what most people think.

Hi [First Name],

Quick question: What do you think it takes to [achieve the result your product delivers]?

Most people say [common wrong answer].

The data (and my [X] years of experience) says something different: [your insight]

[3-4 sentences explaining why the common belief is wrong and what actually works]

This is the foundation of what I'm releasing on [date].

It won't be for everyone — but if [target criteria], it was built for you.

[Name]

---

[LS-03] PRE-LAUNCH EMAIL 3 — THE CASE STUDY (3 days before)

SUBJECT: How [Client Type] went from [Before] to [After] in [Timeframe]
PREVIEW TEXT: The exact [method/system/approach] that made it happen.

Hi [First Name],

Let me tell you about [client description — anonymous or named].

[3-4 sentence story: their situation before, what they tried, what wasn't working]

They started using [your approach] [timeframe] ago.

Here's what changed:
→ [Specific result 1]
→ [Specific result 2]
→ [Specific result 3]

What made the difference: [Core insight about your approach — 2-3 sentences]

I'm making the [system/approach/tool] available to you on [date].

Details tomorrow.

[Name]

[Button/Link]: GET [PRODUCT NAME] NOW →

[Scarcity/urgency element if applicable: "Founding member pricing ends [date]" or "Only [X] copies at launch price"]

[Name]

[NE-01] THE "ONE THING" EMAIL

---

Subject: [What happened when I tried [thing]]
Format: A short personal story that teaches a lesson.
Structure: Story setup → What happened → What I learned → Reader takeaway

---

Structure: Brief intro → List with one sentence per item → Sign off

---

[NE-04] THE QUESTION EMAIL
Subject: [Quick question for you]

Structure: Why I'm asking → The question → [Offer to share what you learn from responses]

---

SECTION 5: EMAIL COPYWRITING GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE 5 ELEMENTS OF AN EMAIL THAT GETS READ:

1. SUBJECT LINE — determines whether it opens
   → Aim for curiosity, personal tone, or specific benefit
   → Test: would you open this if someone else sent it?

2. PREVIEW TEXT — 40-100 characters after subject
   → Continue the subject line intrigue
   → Don't repeat the subject line
   → Make it a reason to open

3. OPENING LINE — determines whether they keep reading
   → Never start with "I" — start with "You" or a statement
   → First sentence = most important sentence
   → Get to the point in the first 10 words

4. BODY — delivers the value or story
   → Short paragraphs (1-3 lines max)
   → One idea per email
   → Conversational tone — write like you talk

5. CTA — what you want them to do
   → One CTA only
   → Specific and low-friction: "Reply", "Click here", "Click to grab"
   → Not in every email — 70/30 rule (value vs. sales)

EMAIL FREQUENCY GUIDELINES:
Daily: Only if each email has standalone value (news, education, personality)
3x/week: Works for active audiences who want volume
Weekly: The sweet spot for most lists
Bi-weekly: Acceptable for lower-frequency senders
Monthly: Minimum to maintain list health

THE 70/30 RULE:
70% value emails (education, stories, entertainment, resources)
30% sales/promotional emails
Don't break this ratio — audiences tune out lists that only sell

LIST HYGIENE:
→ Remove subscribers who haven't opened in 6 months (after re-engagement attempt)
→ Clean your list quarterly
→ Low open rates (below 20%) hurt deliverability
→ A smaller engaged list beats a large unengaged list

DELIVERABILITY BASICS:
→ Use a custom sending domain (not gmail.com for marketing)
→ SPF, DKIM, DMARC records set up
→ Don't use spam trigger words: "FREE!!!", "Click here", "100% guaranteed"
→ Text-to-image ratio: more text than images
→ Unsubscribe link always present
