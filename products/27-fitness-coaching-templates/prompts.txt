CALORIE & MACRO CALCULATOR:
[NC-01] TDEE CALCULATION:
BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor):
Male: 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5
Female: 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age − 161

Activity multiplier:
Sedentary (desk job, minimal exercise): × 1.2
Light activity (1-3 days/week exercise): × 1.375
Moderate (3-5 days/week): × 1.55
Very active (6-7 days/week): × 1.725
Extremely active (athlete/2x daily): × 1.9

TDEE = BMR × activity multiplier

CALORIE TARGETS BY GOAL:
Weight loss: TDEE − 300 to 500 (0.5-1 lb/week)
Aggressive loss: TDEE − 750 (don't go below 1,200 women / 1,500 men)
Maintenance: TDEE
Muscle gain (lean bulk): TDEE + 200 to 300

MACRO BREAKDOWN TEMPLATES:
Fat loss focus: Protein 40% / Carbs 35% / Fat 25%
Muscle gain: Protein 30% / Carbs 45% / Fat 25%
Balanced: Protein 30% / Carbs 40% / Fat 30%
Low carb: Protein 35% / Carbs 25% / Fat 40%
