MONEY COACHING QUESTIONS (50 by topic):

MINDSET QUESTIONS:
[MQ-01] "What did money mean in your household growing up?"
[MQ-02] "What's your earliest money memory?"
[MQ-03] "What feelings come up when you check your bank account?"
[MQ-04] "If money were a person, what would your relationship look like?"
[MQ-05] "What do you believe you deserve financially?"
[MQ-06] "Who taught you that [limiting money belief]?"
[MQ-07] "What would change about your life if money wasn't a worry?"
[MQ-08] "When do you feel most financially confident?"
[MQ-09] "What's the story you tell yourself about why you're not further ahead?"
[MQ-10] "What would the 'financially healthy version of you' do differently?"

BEHAVIOR QUESTIONS:
[MQ-11] "When do you spend emotionally vs. intentionally?"
[MQ-12] "What's your biggest money habit you want to change?"
[MQ-13] "What does a 'treat yourself' purchase usually look like?"
[MQ-14] "When did you last make a financial decision you regret?"
[MQ-15] "What's one area where you're already good with money?"

GOAL QUESTIONS:
[MQ-16] "What does financial freedom mean to you specifically?"
[MQ-17] "What number would make you feel safe? Why that number?"
[MQ-18] "What would you do with your time if you didn't need money?"
[MQ-19] "What's your financial 'enough'?"

DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT:
[DC-01] Opening: "Tell me what's going on financially right now — the real picture."
[DC-02] Deep dive: "What have you tried? What's not working? What are you most afraid of?"
[DC-03] Vision: "What would your life look like if this was solved?"
[DC-04] Stakes: "What happens financially if nothing changes in 5 years?"
[DC-05] Offer presentation: "Based on what you've shared, here's how I can help..."
[DC-06] Close: "Does this feel like the right step for you right now?"
