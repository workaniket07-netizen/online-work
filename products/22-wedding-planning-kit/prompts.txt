SECTION 1: MASTER WEDDING CHECKLIST (12-Month Timeline)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

12 MONTHS OUT:
□ Set overall wedding budget
□ Determine approximate guest count (affects every vendor)
□ Choose wedding date (or narrow to 2-3 options)
□ Discuss wedding vision/style with partner
□ Research and book ceremony venue
□ Research and book reception venue
□ Research and book wedding photographer (books out 12+ months)
□ Research and book videographer
□ Start building wedding website
□ Create wedding email address for vendor communication
□ Create shared planning folder (Google Drive/Notion)
□ Begin engagement photos (optional but recommended now)
□ Announce engagement — Instagram, family calls

10 MONTHS OUT:
□ Book officiant
□ Begin wedding dress/attire shopping (alterations take 4-6 months)
□ Book caterer (if separate from venue)
□ Research florist options
□ Research and book band or DJ
□ Create preliminary guest list
□ Research and book wedding planner/day-of coordinator (optional)
□ Begin honeymoon research and booking
□ Set up wedding registry (department store + online)
□ Send save-the-dates (for destination weddings: 12 months out)

8 MONTHS OUT:
□ Book florist
□ Book hair and makeup artists
□ Book cake designer/baker
□ Book transportation (limo, trolley, shuttle)
□ Book rehearsal dinner venue
□ Finalize guest list
□ Choose and ask bridesmaids/groomsmen/wedding party
□ Begin researching rehearsal dinner caterer
□ Schedule venue walk-through
□ Research and purchase wedding insurance

6 MONTHS OUT:
□ Order wedding dress
□ Order bridesmaid dresses
□ Choose suits/attire for groom and groomsmen
□ Book hotel room block for out-of-town guests
□ Create wedding website (if not done — must be done before invitations)
□ Design and order invitations (send 6-8 weeks before wedding)
□ Begin honeymoon visa/passport check
□ Register for gifts (add more items if registry sparse)
□ Begin planning honeymoon in detail
□ Book honeymoon travel and accommodation
□ Schedule dress fitting appointment
□ Plan rehearsal dinner menu and invitations

4 MONTHS OUT:
□ Mail invitations (aim for 6-8 weeks before wedding)
□ Finalize ceremony music/songs
□ Write wedding vows (or begin if writing own)
□ Plan ceremony processional order
□ Create seating chart (draft — refine as RSVPs come in)
□ Schedule final dress fitting
□ Choose wedding rings (allow 6-8 weeks for custom)
□ Finalize honeymoon itinerary
□ Buy wedding shoes and accessories
□ Break in wedding shoes
□ Plan bridal shower (bridesmaids typically lead)
□ Plan bachelor/bachelorette parties

2 MONTHS OUT:
□ RSVP deadline — follow up with non-respondents
□ Finalize guest count to all vendors
□ Finalize seating chart
□ Create detailed day-of timeline (share with vendors + wedding party)
□ Confirm all vendors (written confirmation)
□ Arrange vendor payments schedule
□ Create vendor contact list and share with planner/coordinator
□ Write ceremony script (if writing own)
□ Buy wedding party gifts
□ Buy parent gifts
□ Purchase wedding favors (if doing)
□ Final dress fitting
□ Apply for marriage license (check your state/country requirements)
□ Arrange name change documentation (if applicable)

1 MONTH OUT:
□ Final venue walk-through with vendors
□ Final meeting with photographer (shot list)
□ Deliver items to venue if allowed early
□ Prepare vendor tips in envelopes (cash)
□ Assign day-of responsibilities to trusted people
□ Confirm honeymoon details — print all documents
□ Prepare emergency kit (below)
□ Write personal vows (if not done)
□ Get marriage license
□ Rehearsal dinner — confirm all details
□ Send final guest list and table assignments to venue

WEDDING WEEK:
□ Final fittings and pick up dress
□ Deliver items to venue
□ Rehearsal + rehearsal dinner
□ Rest. Actually rest.
□ Last-minute vendor confirmations
□ Prepare wedding morning bag
□ Confirm day-of timeline with wedding party

WEDDING DAY:
□ Eat breakfast
□ Stick to timeline
□ Trust your vendors
□ Be present
□ Get married

POST-WEDDING:
□ Return rentals within agreed timeframe
□ Send wedding thank you notes (within 3 months — track with tracker below)
□ Return/preserve wedding dress
□ File name change paperwork
□ Write vendor reviews (Google, The Knot, WeddingWire)
□ Download and back up all photos/videos
□ Create wedding album (order within 3 months)

SEATING CHART SYSTEM:
[SC-01] Group guests by: immediate family, extended family, friends (bride's side), friends (groom's side), work colleagues, family friends
[SC-02] Seat family at closest tables to head table
[SC-03] Mix sides gently (not required but encouraged for community feel)
[SC-04] Seat elderly guests near exits and away from speakers
[SC-05] Seat children/families near each other and near restrooms
[SC-06] Buffer empty seats at tables for late RSVPs
[SC-07] Don't seat known conflicting guests together

WEDDING VOWS FRAMEWORKS:
[WV-01] TRADITIONAL STYLE:
"I, [Name], take you, [Partner's Name], to be my [husband/wife/partner].
I promise to love you completely, to stand by you in times of joy and sorrow, to grow with you, and to be your faithful partner in all things.
I give you my hand and my heart as I take you as my [husband/wife/partner] from this day forward."

[WV-02] PERSONAL/HEARTFELT STYLE:
"[Partner's Name], from the moment [specific memory], I knew I wanted to spend my life with you.
You are [3 specific qualities you love about them].
I promise to [specific promise 1], to [specific promise 2], and to [specific promise 3].
I love you today, tomorrow, and for the rest of our lives together."

[WV-03] HUMOROUS + HEARTFELT:
"[Partner's Name], when I told people I was going to marry you, everyone said [funny thing that's also sweet].
They were right.
I promise to [funny but real promise], to [another real promise], and [heartfelt promise].
You make me [how they make you feel] every single day. And for that, I am the luckiest [man/woman/person] alive.
I love you."

[WV-04] SHORT AND POWERFUL:
"[Partner's Name] — you are my home. My best friend. The person I choose every day.
I promise to love you fully, to always be in your corner, and to choose us — even when it's hard.
Forever and always."

[WV-05] RELIGIOUS/SPIRITUAL STYLE:
Include: gratitude for partnership, commitment before [God/higher power/community], specific promises, intention for the marriage
