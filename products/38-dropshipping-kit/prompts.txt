WINNING PRODUCT CRITERIA (score 1 point each):
□ Solves a clear problem or triggers strong emotion
□ Not easily found in local stores (drives online purchase)
□ Sell price $127-150 (sweet spot for impulse + low return rate)
□ Light and small (low shipping cost)
□ Trending upward on Google Trends (not declining)
□ 2-4x profit margin possible
□ Supplier ships in under 2 weeks (ideally 7-10 days)
□ Not heavily branded/trademarked
Score 6+: Strong product. Score 4-5: Needs more validation. Under 4: Skip.

CUSTOMER SERVICE SCRIPTS (30):
[CS-01] "Where is my order?": Check tracking → provide link → if delayed, apologize + offer timeline
[CS-02] "My order never arrived": Check tracking → file claim with carrier → reship or refund
[CS-03] "Wrong item received": Apologize + ship correct item immediately + let them keep wrong item (cheaper than return shipping)
[CS-04] "Item broken/defective": Apologize + reship immediately + no return required
[CS-05] "I want to cancel": If not shipped: cancel immediately. If shipped: explain return process.
[CS-06] "Is this product real?": Address trust concerns with evidence (reviews, before/after, guarantee)
[CS-07 to CS-30] More: dispute responses, chargeback prevention, international customs, delay explanation
