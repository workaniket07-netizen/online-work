PROMPT CHAIN — CONTENT REPURPOSING:

[CA-01] Blog to LinkedIn:
"Take this blog post and extract the 5 most powerful insights. Rewrite each as a standalone LinkedIn post using this format: bold opening line → 3-4 short paragraphs → engaging question at end. Each post should be independent and not require reading the original. Blog post: [PASTE]"

[CA-02] Blog to Twitter/X Thread:
"Convert this blog post into a Twitter thread of 8-12 tweets. Tweet 1: powerful hook that makes people want to read more. Tweets 2-10: one key point each with concrete detail. Final tweet: summary + call to action. Each tweet under 280 chars. Use thread numbering (1/, 2/, etc.). Blog: [PASTE]"

[CA-03] Blog to Email Newsletter:
"Convert this blog post into a weekly email newsletter. Format: compelling subject line (5 options) + preview text + personal intro (2 sentences) + the key insight from the post + one actionable tip + soft CTA. Max 400 words. Conversational tone. Blog: [PASTE]"

[CA-04] Video/Podcast to Instagram:
"I have a transcript from my [video/podcast]. Extract 5 quotable moments that would work as Instagram posts. For each: format as a quote graphic (bold quote + attribution) + a 2-3 sentence caption + 5 relevant hashtags. Transcript: [PASTE]"

[CA-05] Any Content to Pinterest:
"Create 5 Pinterest pin descriptions for content about [TOPIC]. Each pin: keyword-rich title (under 100 chars) + description (150-300 chars) with value proposition + 5 hashtag keywords. Target audience: [AUDIENCE]. Main keyword: [KEYWORD]"

EMAIL TRIAGE SYSTEM:
Use AI to categorize, draft, and respond to emails in bulk

[EA-01] Email response generator:
"I need to reply to this email professionally. Context about my business: [BUSINESS CONTEXT]. The email: [PASTE EMAIL]. Write 3 response options: 1) Quick acknowledgment + next step 2) Detailed helpful response 3) Decline/redirect response. I'll choose one."

[EA-02] Email template library builder:
"I run a [BUSINESS TYPE]. Generate 10 email templates I'll need regularly: welcome new customer, order confirmation, follow-up after no response, refund request response, complaint acknowledgment, thank you for purchase, re-engagement, referral ask, review request, partnership inquiry response."

[EA-03] Customer complaint → response:
"Customer complaint: [PASTE]. Write a professional, empathetic response that: acknowledges the issue, takes appropriate responsibility, offers a concrete solution, and leaves them feeling valued. Don't be overly apologetic or robotic. Tone: human and warm."

[EA-04] Cold email to warm:
"This is a cold email I wrote: [PASTE]. Make it warmer, more personal, and more likely to get a response by: adding a specific observation about the recipient, leading with value not ask, making the CTA super easy to respond to. Keep it under 100 words."

[EA-05] Email A/B test variants:
"Write 5 different versions of this email with different: subject lines, opening lines, and CTAs. Keep the core message the same but vary the angle: curiosity, benefit, urgency, social proof, direct. Original: [PASTE]"

[RA-01] Competitor analysis in 10 minutes:
"Act as a business strategist. Analyze [COMPETITOR] based on publicly available information. Cover: their positioning, target customer, top products/services, pricing strategy, marketing channels, apparent weaknesses, and what they're NOT doing that creates opportunity. Output as structured report."

[RA-02] Market research shortcut:
"I'm entering the [MARKET/NICHE] market. Give me: top 5 customer pain points, top 5 unmet desires, 3 common objections to buying, what customers currently use as alternatives, key keywords they search, and 5 content topics that would attract them."

[RA-03] Industry trend analysis:
"What are the top 5 emerging trends in [INDUSTRY] in 2025? For each: what it is, why it's growing, which businesses it affects most, and what to do about it in the next 6 months."

[RA-04] Customer interview synthesizer:
"I have these notes from 10 customer interviews: [PASTE]. Synthesize into: top 3 themes, exact language customers use, primary pain point, what they wish existed, and 3 product/service ideas based on their feedback."

[RA-05] Data interpretation:
"Here's data from my business analytics: [PASTE DATA]. Identify: top 3 insights, what's working, what's underperforming, anomalies worth investigating, and 3 specific actions I should take based on this data."

[SA-01] Lead research in 2 minutes:
"Based on this LinkedIn profile: [PASTE]. Tell me: what business problems they likely face, what they probably want to achieve, how to open a conversation, and what product/service would genuinely help them."

[SA-02] Proposal generator:
"Create a business proposal for [CLIENT TYPE] for the service [SERVICE]. Include: executive summary, problem statement, proposed solution, implementation plan, timeline, and investment tiers (3 options). Make it persuasive but not salesy."

[SA-03] Objection crusher:
"Prospect said: '[OBJECTION]'. I sell [PRODUCT/SERVICE] at [PRICE]. Give me 5 different responses to this objection, ranging from: empathetic reframe → evidence-based → alternative offer → direct address → story response."

[SA-04] Follow-up sequence:
"Create a 5-touch follow-up sequence for a prospect who: attended a demo but didn't buy. Touch 1 (same day): recap value. Touch 2 (day 3): address likely concern. Touch 3 (day 7): share relevant success story. Touch 4 (day 14): add new value. Touch 5 (day 21): final check-in."

[SA-05] Price increase script:
"I need to raise my price from [CURRENT] to [NEW] for existing clients. Write: a value-first email announcing this, a phone script for when they push back, and a retention offer for clients who say they want to leave."

[SA-06 to SA-75] Full sales automation:
Discovery call preparation, deal review analysis, win-loss analysis, upsell scripts, referral asks, account expansion strategies
