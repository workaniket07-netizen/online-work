THE 7-DAY LAUNCH FORMULA (for any AI service):

DAY 1 — PICK AND VALIDATE
Choose hustle from the list. Validate by:
→ Search "[your service] freelance" on Upwork — see current jobs and rates
→ Search "[your service]" on LinkedIn — see who's buying
→ Search "[your service] template" on Gumroad — see what sells
Green light: 5+ active jobs OR 3+ products selling → proceed

DAY 2 — BUILD YOUR PROOF
Create 3 work samples using AI tools. These are your portfolio.
For service businesses: Create before/after examples
For digital products: Create one complete product
Quality bar: Would a busy professional pay $X for this? If yes: good. If no: improve.

DAY 3 — SET YOUR OFFER
Pick ONE service/product — not five.
Price it simply: One deliverable, one clear price.
Create a simple one-page Google Doc or Notion page describing:
→ What you do
→ Who it's for
→ What they get
→ Price + turnaround
→ Contact info

DAY 4 — GET IN FRONT OF BUYERS
Choose ONE channel. Go deep, not wide:
Channel A — Upwork: Create profile, apply to 10 relevant jobs
Channel B — LinkedIn: DM 20 potential clients
Channel C — Twitter/X: Post daily about your service + results
Channel D — Cold email: Send 30 personalized emails
Channel E — Reddit: Answer questions in relevant subreddits + mention your service

DAY 5-6 — FOLLOW UP + REFINE
Send follow-ups to everyone from Day 4.
Adjust pitch based on what gets responses.
The pitch that gets 10% reply rate = keep. Below 5% = rewrite.

DAY 7 — FIRST PAYMENT
Target: Get paid for something — even $127.
Lower your price if needed to get the first win.
First client unlocks: testimonial, referral, confidence, feedback.
