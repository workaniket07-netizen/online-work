SECTION 1: GRANT APPLICATION COMPONENTS (100 Templates)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY TEMPLATE:
[Organization name] requests $[amount] from [Funder name] to [brief project description] in [location] over [timeframe].

We will [specific action] to [specific outcome] for [target population]. This project directly aligns with [funder's priority area] by [connection to their mission].

[Organization] has [evidence of capacity: years in operation, past successful projects, community relationships]. With this grant, we will [single most compelling outcome — make it quantifiable if possible].

NEED STATEMENT / STATEMENT OF NEED (20 templates):
[NS-01] Data-driven opening: "In [geographic area], [problem] affects [X number/percent] of [population]. This means [human impact translation] — [specific consequence]. Despite [what exists], [gap] remains unaddressed."
[NS-02] Story-first opening: "[Brief story about one person affected by the problem — 3-4 sentences]. [Name]'s situation is not unique. [Statistic connecting the story to the larger problem]."
[NS-03] Trend-based: "Over the past [X years], [problem] in [area] has increased by [X%]. Without intervention, [projection]. [Organization]'s program addresses this trend by [solution]."

PROJECT DESCRIPTION (10 templates):
[PD-01] Activities-based: "The [Project Name] will deliver [activities] to [target population] through [methods]. Activities include: [list]. Each activity is evidence-based, having shown [outcome] in [research/prior programs]."
[PD-02] Theory of change: "If we [inputs/activities], then [outputs] will occur. These outputs will lead to [outcomes] because [logic]. Ultimately, this contributes to [long-term impact]."
[PD-03] SMART goal format: "By [date], [Organization] will [specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound outcome]."

BUDGET NARRATIVE (15 templates):
[BN-01] Personnel: "[Title] (1.0 FTE): $[X]. This position is responsible for [specific duties related to grant]. Their [qualification] makes them uniquely positioned for [specific tasks]."
[BN-02] Contractual: "We will contract with [type of organization] for [service]. This approach is preferred because [cost-effectiveness / expertise / community trust]. Cost: $[X] based on [rate × units]."
[BN-03] Indirect/overhead: "Indirect costs at [X]% of [base] = $[X]. These costs represent [what they cover] and are documented in our [cost allocation plan / negotiated indirect rate agreement]."

EVALUATION PLAN (10 templates):
[EP-01] Process evaluation: "We will track [activities completed], [people served], [quality indicators]. Data collected via [method] [frequency]. Responsible: [position]."
[EP-02] Outcome evaluation: "We will measure [specific outcome] using [instrument] at [baseline / mid / post program]. Target: [X]% of participants will [achieve outcome]. Analysis conducted by [who]."
[EP-03] Logic model components: Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Short-term outcomes → Long-term outcomes → Impact

SUSTAINABILITY SECTION (10 templates):
[SS-01] Diversified funding: "After this grant period, [Project] will be sustained through [X sources]: [revenue X% / government contract X% / earned income X%]. We have [evidence of existing commitments]."
[SS-02] Capacity building: "This grant will [build staff capacity / develop systems / establish partnerships] that will continue beyond the grant period regardless of this funder's continued support."

LOI (LETTER OF INQUIRY) TEMPLATES (10):
[LI-01] "Dear [Funder name], [Organization] seeks [amount] from [Foundation] to [project description]. This request aligns with your priority of [their language] through [how our work addresses it]. We have served [population] for [X years], achieving [key outcome]. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss this further and attach our LOI for your review."

SITE VISIT PREPARATION:
[SV-01] Before: Review your application; prepare to speak to any section without notes; have data ready; prepare story of one client (with permission); know your budget deeply
[SV-02] During: Lead with mission impact, not organizational needs; answer questions concisely; show passion without begging
[SV-03] After: Send thank-you within 24 hours; provide any requested follow-up information promptly

DONOR/FUNDER RELATIONSHIP CALENDAR:
[RC-01] Regular touchpoints: 1-2 updates per year even when not applying
[RC-02] Grant report → natural opportunity to share success
[RC-03] Invite funders to events and program visits
[RC-04] Send relevant news articles that connect to their interests

[GP-01] "Write a need statement for a grant application about [program/issue] in [location]. Use this data: [paste statistics]. Include: the scale of the problem, who is most affected, why existing solutions fall short, and urgency."

[GP-02] "Review this grant narrative for: alignment with the funder's priorities (their priorities: [list]), clarity of outcomes, strength of evidence cited, and persuasiveness. Suggest specific improvements: [paste narrative]."

[GP-03] "Write a project description for a [program type] serving [population] in [location]. Program activities: [list]. Our evidence base: [research/prior outcomes]. Funder priority: [their language]. Length: [X words]."

[GP-04] "Transform this impact story into a compelling narrative for a grant report: [facts about the client/situation]. Use real storytelling techniques — opening scene, tension, resolution. Client is anonymized as [pseudonym]."

[GP-05] "Create a logic model for this program: Goal: [goal]. Activities: [list]. Target population: [description]. Expected outcomes: [list]. Format as a table with: Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Short-term outcomes → Long-term outcomes → Impact."
