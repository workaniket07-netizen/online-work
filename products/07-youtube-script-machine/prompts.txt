SECTION 1: VIDEO HOOKS (60 Templates)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HOOKS are the first 3-10 seconds. They determine if viewers stay or scroll.
These are the 7 proven hook types — 60 ready-to-use scripts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TYPE 1: THE BOLD CLAIM HOOK (8 hooks)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[H-01] "[Shocking number/claim] that [target audience] never talk about — until now."
Example: "The one copywriting trick that generated $127,000 in a weekend that no one talks about — until now."

[H-02] "I [impressive result] in [short timeframe] — here's exactly how."
Example: "I went from 0 to 10,000 subscribers in 90 days — here's exactly how."

[H-03] "Everything you know about [topic] is wrong. Here's what actually works."
Example: "Everything you know about morning routines is wrong. Here's what actually works."

[H-04] "I quit [mainstream approach] and [surprising outcome]. Here's my honest review."
Example: "I quit social media for 60 days and my business grew 40%. Here's my honest breakdown."

[H-05] "[Expert/popular source] says [claim] — but they're leaving out the most important part."
Example: "Every productivity guru says wake up at 5am — but they're leaving out the most important part."

[H-06] "This [simple thing] made me [impressive result]. I wish I'd known this [X] years ago."
Example: "This one daily habit made me $200k last year. I wish I'd known this 5 years ago."

[H-07] "If you do [common thing], you're accidentally [negative consequence]. Stop now."
Example: "If you're posting on Instagram every day, you're accidentally killing your reach. Stop now."

[H-08] "In the next [X minutes], I'll show you [specific outcome]."
Example: "In the next 8 minutes, I'll show you how to write a landing page that converts at 40%."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TYPE 2: THE STORY HOOK (8 hooks)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[H-09] "[Time ago], I [low point/failure]. Today, [impressive contrast]. This is what changed."
Example: "Two years ago, I was $127k in debt with a failing business. Today, I'm debt-free and earning $127k/month. This is what changed."

[H-10] "I made a huge mistake with [topic]. I'm sharing it so you don't have to."
Example: "I made a huge mistake investing in crypto last year. I'm sharing it so you don't have to."

[H-11] "Nobody told me [hard truth] when I started [thing]. So I'm telling you."
Example: "Nobody told me how lonely entrepreneurship would be when I started. So I'm telling you."

[H-12] "I tried [popular thing] for [timeframe]. Here's what actually happened."
Example: "I tried waking up at 4am for 30 days. Here's what actually happened to my productivity."

[H-13] "My [mentor / hero / person I admire] told me [counterintuitive advice]. They were right."
Example: "My first boss told me to stop trying to be liked by clients. He was completely right."

[H-14] "The worst [experience] of my [professional/personal] life taught me [lesson]."
Example: "The worst client I ever had taught me the most valuable lesson about business."

[H-15] "Last [month/year], I [specific decision]. Best or worst decision? Watch to find out."
Example: "Last year I fired my biggest client. Best or worst business decision of my life? Watch to find out."

[H-16] "The day I realized [paradigm-shifting insight] changed everything for me."
Example: "The day I realized I was building a job instead of a business changed everything for me."

FRAMEWORK A: THE LISTICLE (10 scripts)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[F-01] THE CLASSIC LISTICLE FRAMEWORK

HOOK (0:00-0:15):
"[Number] [things] that [target outcome/emotion]. Let me break them down."

CREDIBILITY + CONTEXT (0:15-0:30):
"I've been [experience in topic] for [timeframe]. These are the [things] I come back to again and again."

ITEM 1 (0:30-1:30):
"[Number] — [Item title]. [Explain the concept in 2-3 sentences. Give one concrete example. Tell them why it matters.]"

[Repeat for each item]

PATTERN INTERRUPT (midpoint):
"Before I get to [remaining number], here's the one that surprises most people..."

FINAL ITEM (penultimate):
"And the most important one — [title]. [Deeper explanation. This one gets more time.]"

CTA CLOSE:
"If [most useful tip] resonated, you'll want to watch [related video]. And if you haven't already — subscribe, I post [frequency] about [topic]. See you in the next one."

---

[F-02] THE RANKED LIST (with data)

HOOK: "I analyzed [data source] to rank the [X best/worst/most effective] [things]. Here's what I found."
METHODOLOGY: "Here's how I ranked them: [criteria 1], [criteria 2], [criteria 3]."
REVERSE ORDER BUILD: Start at #[X], work up to #1 for maximum retention.
REVEAL: "#1 might surprise you — it's not what most people expect."
CTA: "Bookmark this one — I'll update it every [timeframe] as the data changes."

---

[F-03] THE "MISTAKES" LIST

HOOK: "[Number] mistakes [target audience] make that are quietly [costing/hurting/limiting] them."
RELATABILITY: "I made every single one of these. Here's what I learned."
MISTAKE FORMAT: "[Mistake name] — [What it looks like] — [Why people do it] — [What to do instead]"
EMPATHY: "Be kind to yourself about these — they're common because [reason]. The goal isn't guilt, it's awareness."
CTA: "If you want to go deeper on [topic], here's my video on [related topic]."

---

[F-04] RESOURCES/TOOLS LIST

HOOK: "[Number] [tools/books/resources] that [specific outcome]. I use all of these."
VETTING: "Quick note — none of these are sponsored. These are what I actually use."
ITEM FORMAT: "[Name] — [What it does] — [How I use it] — [Price/access] — [Who it's for]"
WINNER REVEAL: "My #1 recommendation for [specific use case] is [name] because [reason]."
CTA: "All links in the description. If you try any of these, let me know in the comments which one helped most."

---

[F-05] THE CONTRARIAN LIST

HOOK: "[Number] pieces of [popular] advice that are actually bad for [target audience]."
TRUST BUILDER: "I believed all of these at some point. This took me [timeframe] to unlearn."
ITEM FORMAT: "Bad advice: [quote/common advice] — Why it's wrong: [reason] — What actually works: [alternative]"
NUANCE: "These aren't universally wrong — but for [specific situation/audience], here's why they fall short."

---

[F-06 to F-10] RAPID LISTICLE FRAMEWORKS

[F-06] The "Quick Tips" List: Each tip under 60 seconds. Value-dense. No fluff. Good for shorts → long-form conversion.
Format: "Tip [#]: [Name] — [One sentence explanation] — [One sentence application] — [One sentence result]"

[F-07] The "Lessons Learned" List: Personal narrative format. Each lesson has a story. Emotional, relatable, shareable.
Format: "Lesson [#]: [What I learned] — [The story that taught me this] — [How you can apply it without the painful experience]"

[F-08] The "Ask the Expert" Compilation: "I asked [X] [experts] for their #1 advice on [topic]. Here's what they said."
Format: Show quote/clip → your commentary → application for viewer

[F-09] The "Before vs. After" List: "[X] things that changed when I [took action/made change]."
Format: "[Area]: Before: [situation] → After: [situation] — What changed: [action]"

[F-10] The "Warning Signs" List: "[X] signs [negative thing] is happening (and how to fix it)."
Format: "Sign #[X]: [What it looks like] — Why this is a problem — What to do immediately"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[F-11] THE STEP-BY-STEP TUTORIAL

SECTION 6: YOUTUBE GROWTH STRATEGY GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE 3 METRICS THAT ACTUALLY MATTER:

1. CLICK-THROUGH RATE (CTR): Target 4-8% for established channels, 6-10% for new channels.
   → Improve with better thumbnails + stronger titles
   → Test 2 thumbnail options per video if possible

2. AVERAGE VIEW DURATION (AVD): Target 40-60% of video length.
   → Improve with faster pacing, pattern interrupts every 90 seconds
   → Cut any segment where you lost energy

3. IMPRESSIONS: How many times YouTube showed your video.
   → High impressions + low CTR = title/thumbnail problem
   → Low impressions = topic/keyword problem

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

VIDEO TYPES AND WHEN TO USE THEM:

SEARCH VIDEOS (evergreen): Answer specific questions. Long shelf life. Grow channel passively.
Use for: How-to, tutorials, explainers, beginner guides
Title pattern: "How to [specific thing]" / "What is [X]" / "[X] explained"

BROWSE VIDEOS (interest-driven): Appear on YouTube homepage. Need strong thumbnail + title.
Use for: Opinion pieces, story videos, trending topics, lists
Thumbnail must work with NO text — curiosity + visual interest is everything

SUGGESTED VIDEOS: Show up after similar content. Need to appeal to existing audiences in your space.
Use for: Reactive content, collaboration adjacent, topic clusters

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

POSTING STRATEGY:

New channel (0-1k subscribers): Post 2x/week. Focus 80% on search videos. Build library.
Growing channel (1k-10k): Post 1-2x/week. Mix search + browse. Find your best-performing format and double down.
Established channel (10k+): Post 1x/week or less. Quality over quantity. Optimize for RPM.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THUMBNAIL FORMULA:
1. HIGH CONTRAST background (not white)
2. YOUR FACE with strong emotion (or the thing — not both)
3. 3-4 WORDS MAXIMUM (if text at all)
4. VISUAL INTRIGUE: Show something people want explained
