HOW PINTEREST SEO WORKS:
Pinterest = visual search engine, not social media.
Content lasts years (unlike Instagram's 24-48 hours).
Best traffic: Free, organic, warm buyers.

KEYWORD RESEARCH METHOD:
Step 1: Type your main topic into Pinterest search bar
Step 2: Note autocomplete suggestions (= what people search)
Step 3: Click on a relevant result
Step 4: Note the "related searches" below → more keywords
Step 5: Save keyword list → use in pin titles, descriptions, board names

PINTEREST KEYWORD PLACEMENT:
Pin title: Primary keyword at start
Pin description: Primary keyword in first sentence + secondary keywords throughout + 5-10 keywords at end
Board name: Keyword-rich (this is how Pinterest indexes you)
Board description: Full sentences with keywords
Profile bio: Top 2-3 keywords for your niche

WEEK 1 — FOUNDATION:
Day 1: Set up profile (keyword-rich bio, professional photo, website link claimed)
Day 2: Create 10-15 boards with keyword-rich names and descriptions
Day 3: Pin 15-20 existing content from your website/Gumroad to relevant boards
Day 4-7: Pin 5-10 new pins per day, mix of your content + relevant repins

WEEK 2 — CONTENT SYSTEM:
Day 8-14: Create 3-5 fresh pins per day (new pin images for existing content)
Test: 3 different title styles per piece of content
Use: Canva templates for consistency and speed

WEEK 3 — OPTIMIZE:
Day 15-21: Check Pinterest Analytics
Top performing pins: Create 3 more pins linking to same content
Underperforming: Try different title formula
Best boards: Add more pins to them

WEEK 4 — SCALE:
Day 22-28: Schedule pins using [Tailwind or Pinterest's native scheduler]
Optimal posting: 10-25 pins/day for fastest growth
Mix: 80% your content, 20% relevant repins

LONG-TERM PINTEREST SYSTEM:
Daily: 10-15 pins (3-5 fresh + rest scheduled)
Weekly: Analyze top 5 pins → create more like them
Monthly: Add new boards for seasonal content
Quarterly: Audit boards — delete low-performer, create new ones

TRAFFIC TO SALES SYSTEM:
Pinterest → Your pin → Your Gumroad/product page → Sale

Pin optimization for sales:
→ Title clearly states what the product is
→ Description says "click to download/access/buy"
→ Image shows what's inside the product
→ Links directly to product page, not homepage
