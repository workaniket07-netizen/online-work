COLD OUTREACH TEMPLATES (20):
[CO-01] LinkedIn DM: "Hi [Name], I help [business owners / coaches / e-commerce brands] with [specific task] so they can focus on [growth activity]. I noticed you [specific observation about their business]. Would saving [X hours/week] on [task] be valuable? Happy to share more."
[CO-02] Email: "Hi [Name], I specialize in supporting [type of business] owners with [specific services]. I saw [specific thing about them] and thought there might be a fit. I have [X] client slots available this month. Worth a quick call?"
[CO-03 to CO-20] More: job board applications (Upwork, Zirtual, Belay), Facebook group approaches, Instagram DMs, referral network asks, speaking at virtual events to get clients

CLIENT DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT:
Opening: "Tell me about your business and what you're currently spending most of your time on."
Qualify: "Which tasks feel most like they're pulling you away from your zone of genius?"
Pain: "What does it cost you — in time or revenue — when you're stuck doing those tasks?"
Present: "Based on what you've shared, here's how I'd support you: [packages + rationale]."
Close: "Does that feel like a good fit? I'd suggest starting with [specific package] for 30 days to test the fit."

[SOP-02] Calendar management SOP: Scheduling rules, buffer time preferences, video call setup
[SOP-03] Social media scheduling SOP: Platform, frequency, approval process, hashtag guide

[SOP-05] Research task SOP: Sources to use, format to deliver findings, how much depth
[SOP-06 to SOP-20] More SOPs created during onboarding that document client preferences

[SA-01] Hire sub-contractors first (not employees)
[SA-02] Document all your processes before hiring (SOPs from Section 2)
[SA-03] Start with overflow work from your existing clients
[SA-04] Contractor agreement essentials: NDA, scope, pay, IP assignment
