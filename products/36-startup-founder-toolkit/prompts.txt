CUSTOMER DISCOVERY INTERVIEW FRAMEWORK (20 templates):
[CD-01] BEFORE THE INTERVIEW:
→ Target interviewee: [Your early adopter profile — be specific]
→ Goal: Learn about their problem, not validate your solution
→ Golden rule: Ask about past behavior, not future intention

OUTREACH TEMPLATE:
"Hi [Name], I'm [building / researching] [category]. I'm not selling anything — I'm doing 20-minute conversations to understand how [type of person] currently handles [problem area]. Would you be open to a call? I'll send you [small thank-you: Amazon gift card / coffee / etc.]"

[CD-02] INTERVIEW STRUCTURE:
Opening (2 min): "Tell me about your current [workflow/situation/challenge]."
Explore the problem (10 min):
→ "Walk me through the last time you [experienced the problem]."
→ "What did you try? What happened?"
→ "How often does this come up?"
→ "What does it cost you — in time, money, frustration?"
Close (3 min): "Who else should I be talking to about this?"
Never: Show your solution. Ask hypothetical questions. Ask "would you use..."

[CD-03] PROBLEM VALIDATION SCORING:
Is this a real problem? (Multiple people describe it without prompting): 1 point
Do they currently spend money solving it?: 1 point
Do they express frustration without prompting?: 1 point
Do they have a workaround (proof they care enough)?: 1 point
Have you heard the same problem from 10+ people in the same segment?: 2 points
Total 5+ = green light to build

PRODUCT-MARKET FIT SIGNALS (20 templates):
[PMF-01] Sean Ellis test: "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?" 40%+ saying "Very disappointed" = PMF signal
[PMF-02] Retention curve: If your cohort retention flattens (instead of going to zero) = PMF signal
[PMF-03] Organic growth: Users referring without incentive = PMF signal
[PMF-04] Willingness to pay: Customers paid without heavy sales effort
[PMF-05] Support requests: Users asking for features that extend (not fix) the product
[PMF-06 to PMF-20] More: usage depth indicators, segment-level PMF analysis, what to do before PMF, pivoting away from non-PMF, when to raise money before/after PMF

THE 10-SLIDE PITCH DECK FRAMEWORK:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SLIDE 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW
[Company name] | [One-liner: We are the [X] for [Y]]
Headline metrics (if applicable): [$X ARR / X users / X% MoM growth]
Logo + clean design

SLIDE 2: PROBLEM
→ Who has this problem (specific person, not "businesses")
→ How big is the pain (cost, frequency, frustration)
→ Why now (what changed that makes this urgent)
→ A story or specific example that makes it real
Avoid: "The market is inefficient" (meaningless)

SLIDE 3: SOLUTION
→ What you built (clear, simple description)
→ How it solves the problem (specific mechanism)
→ A screenshot or demo if you have one
→ One-sentence answer to "why does this work?"
Avoid: Feature list. Focus on the transformation.

SLIDE 4: MARKET SIZE
→ TAM (total addressable market) with source
→ SAM (serviceable addressable market) — realistic near-term
→ SOM (serviceable obtainable market) — your 3-year target
→ Show your work — don't just cite a report
Credibility test: Can you defend every number?

SLIDE 5: TRACTION
→ Key metrics that show momentum (revenue, users, retention, growth rate)
→ Notable milestones (first paid customer, partnerships, press)
→ The trend line — showing trajectory is more important than absolute number
Early stage: Customer testimonials + pilot results if no metrics yet

SLIDE 6: BUSINESS MODEL
→ How you make money (pricing, unit economics)
→ LTV and CAC if known
→ Revenue model: [subscription / usage-based / marketplace / etc.]
→ How the model gets better with scale

SLIDE 7: GO-TO-MARKET
→ Who your first 100 customers are (specific description)
→ How you reach them (channels with evidence they work)
→ Sales motion: self-serve, inside sales, enterprise?
→ What you've tried and what's working

SLIDE 8: TEAM
→ Why you: domain expertise, unfair advantages, relevant experience
→ Co-founders: complementary skills (avoid redundancy)
→ Key advisors if notable
→ What roles you need to hire next

SLIDE 9: FINANCIALS + PROJECTIONS
→ Current ARR/MRR (if applicable)
→ 18-24 month projections (with assumptions visible)
→ Burn rate and runway
→ Unit economics (if positive or path to positive)

SLIDE 10: THE ASK
→ How much you're raising
→ What it's for (specific milestones it achieves)
→ What you'll have accomplished in 18 months with this capital
→ Contact information
