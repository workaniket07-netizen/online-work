━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ANNUAL APPEAL LETTER FORMULA:
1. Story (emotional hook — one person, specific situation)
2. Problem scale (extend the story to the larger issue)
3. Your organization's solution (brief, evidence-based)
4. Specific impact of gift (what $127/$127/$100/$500 does)
5. Ask (specific ask + deadline if applicable)
6. Easy giving instructions
7. Gratitude

MASTER APPEAL LETTER TEMPLATE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Dear [Donor first name],

[Opening story — 2-3 sentences about ONE specific person, animal, family, community member you serve. Use their name (or pseudonym). Make it specific and human.]

[Transition sentence connecting this story to the larger problem your organization addresses. Include one statistic.]

[Organization name] is changing that — one [person/animal/family] at a time. Last year, we [specific accomplishment with number]. This year, with your support, we'll [next goal].

Here's what your gift does:
→ $127 [specific impact]
→ $127 [specific impact]
→ $100 [specific impact]
→ $500 [specific impact]

[CTA]: Please make your gift today at [URL] or by returning the enclosed reply form by [date].

[Name from your story] is counting on us. And so is [X number] more just like them.

With deep gratitude,
[Executive Director name]
[Title]

P.S. [Repeat the ask + urgency — the P.S. is the most read part after the opening]

THANK YOU LETTER TEMPLATES (20):
[TY-01] First-time donor: "Thank you for your first gift to [Org]! You've joined [X] others who believe [mission statement]. Here's what your $[X] makes possible: [specific impact]. Welcome to our community."
[TY-02] Recurring donor: "Your monthly support means everything — because you give month after month, we can [plan ahead / commit to more staff / expand services]. You make that possible."
[TY-03] Major donor: Personalized, specific, warm. Reference their specific gift and impact. Include a personal note from executive director.
[TY-04] In-kind donation: "Thank you for donating [item]. For our [program/people], this means [specific impact]. Donors like you make it possible for us to [stretch every dollar]."
[TY-05 to TY-20] More: volunteer thank you, board member thank you, corporate donor, tribute gift, memorial gift, grant funder thank you, event sponsor, tribute to honoree, planned gift commitment

DONOR RECOGNITION SYSTEM:
[DR-01] Acknowledgment timeline: Send within 24-48 hours (first-time) / 72 hours (recurring) / same day (major gift)
[DR-02] Donor levels: Name each level meaningfully (impact-related, not generic)
[DR-03] Annual recognition: Report back impact of cumulative giving
[DR-04] Personal touchpoints: Phone call to donors who give $500+ | Handwritten note for $127,000+

DONOR COMMUNICATION CALENDAR:
January: Impact report (what their 2024 gift accomplished)
March: Spring campaign + program update
May: Volunteer/event opportunity
July: Mid-year update (no ask)
September: Fall appeal preview or impact story
November: Giving Tuesday campaign
December: Year-end appeal

AUCTION ITEM PROCUREMENT SCRIPT:
"Hi [Name/Business], my name is [X] from [Organization]. We serve [mission in one sentence]. On [date], we're hosting [event] to raise funds for [program]. We'd be honored if [Business] would donate [item/service/gift card] for our auction. All donors are recognized in our program and on social media. May I send you our donation form?"
