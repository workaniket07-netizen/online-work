PRICING GUIDE STRUCTURE:
[PG-01] Collection names (not package A/B/C — name by experience/outcome)
[PG-02] Three-tier pricing: Entry / Core / Premium
[PG-03] What's included in each tier
[PG-04] Recommended anchor: Make middle tier most appealing
[PG-05] Investment language: "Starting investment" not "price starts at"

PHOTOGRAPHY PRICING GUIDE COPY:
"[Studio/Photographer name] Pricing Guide

Every session is a story. Here's how we tell yours.

PORTRAIT SESSION: $[X]
[X-hour session] | [X] fully edited images | [online gallery for X days]
Best for: [families, individuals, couples]
What you'll experience: [brief evocative description of session experience]

EXTENDED SESSION: $[X]
[X-hour session] | [X] fully edited images | [print credit worth $X] | [extras]
Best for: [milestone moments, larger families]

FULL EXPERIENCE: $[X]
Full day / [X hours] | [X] fully edited images | [all extras] | [album/prints included]

Add-ons:
Extra images: $[X]/image | Rush editing: $[X] | Additional hour: $[X]"

CLIENT INQUIRY RESPONSE (5 templates):
[CI-01] Same-day response: "Hi [Name]! Thank you so much for reaching out. I'd love to capture [their occasion]. I have [availability on dates mentioned]. I'll attach my pricing guide — let me know if you'd like to schedule a quick call!"
[CI-02] Availability check: "Hi [Name], thank you for your interest! I'm [available/unavailable] on [their date]. [If available]: Let me send over details. [If unavailable]: I'd love to refer you to a colleague or check nearby dates."
[CI-03 to CI-05] Follow-up, last-chance, referral alternative

BOOKING QUESTIONNAIRE (20 questions):
[BQ-01] What's the occasion/event you'd like photographed?
[BQ-02] Preferred date(s) and time of day?
[BQ-03] Location preference (indoor studio / outdoor / your home / venue)?
[BQ-04] How many people will be in the session?
[BQ-05] Any children? If so, ages?
[BQ-06] Preferred style: [classic / lifestyle / documentary / fine art]?
[BQ-07] What do you want to do with your photos? (album, wall art, digital only?)
[BQ-08] Any specific shots that are most important to you?
[BQ-09] Have you been photographed professionally before? What did/didn't work?
[BQ-10] Budget range you're comfortable with?
[BQ-11 to BQ-20] Wardrobe help questions, location suggestions needed, special requests, how they found you, referral source

SESSION PREP EMAIL (send 1 week before):
Subject: Your session is [X days] away — everything you need to know!

Hi [Name],

So excited to photograph [you/your family] on [date]!

A few things to make your session amazing:

📍 LOCATION: [Address + parking instructions + what to look for]
🕐 ARRIVAL: Please arrive [5-10 minutes early] to [settle in / find parking / get comfortable]
👗 WHAT TO WEAR:
→ [Style suggestion based on their session type]
→ Colors that photograph well: [earth tones / soft blues / etc.]
→ Avoid: [busy patterns / all black / matching outfits that look costume-y]
→ [Link to your Pinterest wardrobe board if you have one]

📸 WHAT TO EXPECT:
[Describe your session experience briefly — sets them at ease]
Sessions with children typically take [X minutes longer] to allow for [settling in/breaks].

💡 TIPS:
→ [Specific tip for their session type: families, newborns, couples, etc.]
→ [How to mentally prepare for being in front of the camera]

Questions? Text/email me anytime: [contact]
So excited to see you on [date]!
[Your name]

SESSION DAY CHECKLIST:
□ Location scouted (know where the light is)
□ Shot list reviewed with client preferences
□ Memory cards formatted
□ Batteries charged (+ extras)
□ Back-up camera body (for paid sessions)
□ Arrival 15 minutes early
□ Client arrival text sent

POST-SESSION EMAIL (send same day):
"Hi [Name], it was such a joy photographing [you/your family] today!
I can already see some beautiful moments in the cards.
Your gallery will be ready in approximately [X business days].
I'll email you a link to your private online gallery with download and ordering options.
Looking forward to sharing your images! — [Name]"

GALLERY DELIVERY EMAIL:
"Your photos are ready! 🎉
Click here to view your private gallery: [link]
Password: [if applicable]
This gallery will be available for [X] days.
[Instructions for downloading / ordering prints]

And if you have a moment, a review on Google means the world to small businesses like mine."

CONTRACT KEY CLAUSES (10):
[CC-01] Retainer: Non-refundable retainer to hold date
[CC-02] Cancellation: Client cancels — retainer forfeit. Photographer cancels — full refund.
[CC-03] Rescheduling: [X] days notice required
[CC-04] Delivery timeline: [X] weeks from session date
[CC-05] Liability: Photographer's liability limited to session fee amount
[CC-06] Image rights: Client gets personal use; photographer keeps copyright and portfolio use rights
[CC-07] Force majeure: Acts of nature / illness rescheduling policy
[CC-08] Second photographer: Policy on bringing an associate
[CC-09] Image selection: How many selects provided; client does/doesn't choose
[CC-10] Social media: Right to share images; tag policy

ANNUAL REVENUE PLANNING:
[RP-01] Sessions per week: [X] | Weeks working per year: [X] | Total sessions: [X]
[RP-02] Average session value: $[X]
[RP-03] Gross annual revenue: [sessions] × [avg value] = $[X]
[RP-04] Target to hit $[X]/year: [sessions needed] at [avg price]
[RP-05] Minimum session fee needed: Calculate based on target income

REFERRAL SYSTEM:
[RF-01] Past client referral: "Know someone who needs portraits? Send them my way — I'll give you [discount/gift/credit] when they book."
[RF-02] Vendor referral partnerships: Hair stylists, wedding planners, newborn boutiques → mutual referral
[RF-03] Styled shoot for portfolio: Partner with vendors → get content → share credit → everyone wins
