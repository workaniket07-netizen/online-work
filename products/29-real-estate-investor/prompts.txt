MOTIVATED SELLER OUTREACH SCRIPTS (20):
[OS-01] DIRECT MAIL LETTER (to absentee owners):
"Dear Property Owner,

My name is [Name] and I'm a local real estate investor looking to buy properties in [area].

I noticed you own [property address] and thought you might be interested in a simple, fast sale without the typical hassles of listing on the market.

If you're interested in selling, I'd be happy to:
→ Make you an all-cash offer within 48 hours
→ Close on your timeline (as fast as 2 weeks)
→ Buy the property as-is — no repairs needed
→ Pay all closing costs

This isn't for everyone, but if you'd like to explore what I could offer, please call or text me at [number].

[Name]
[Phone] | [Email]"

[OS-02] COLD CALL SCRIPT (to FSBO / expired listings):
"Hi, this is [Name] — I'm a local investor. I saw your property at [address] and wanted to reach out. Are you still considering offers on it?

[If yes]: Great — can I ask a few questions to see if we might be a fit? [Motivation / timeline / condition / price expectations]

[If maybe]: Totally understand. What's the situation with the property? [Listen — discover motivation]

[If no]: Completely fine. If circumstances change, I hope you'll keep me in mind. What's the best way to reach you?"

[OS-03] TEXT MESSAGE TO FSBO:
"Hi! I saw your home at [address] listed for sale. I'm a cash buyer in [area] — no agents, no showings, quick close. Would you be open to a direct offer? — [Name]"
