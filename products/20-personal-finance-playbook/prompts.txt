BUDGET METHODS — WHICH TO USE:

[BT-01] THE 50/30/20 RULE (for beginners):
50% → Needs (housing, food, bills, transport)
30% → Wants (dining, entertainment, lifestyle)
20% → Savings + debt payoff
Best for: First budget, simple income situation

[BT-02] ZERO-BASED BUDGET (most powerful):
Every dollar assigned a job. Income - all categories = $127.
Best for: Paying off debt, people who want total control

[BT-03] THE ENVELOPE METHOD (cash version):
Physical/digital envelopes for each spending category.
When envelope empty = done spending in that category.
Best for: People who overspend in specific areas

[BT-04] PAY YOURSELF FIRST:
Transfer savings the moment you get paid — before anything.
Live on what's left.
Best for: Wealth builders, people who struggle to save

[BT-05] 80/20 BUDGET (for high earners):
Save 20% automatically. Spend rest however you want.
Best for: High earners who don't want to track every dollar

DEBT PAYOFF METHODS:

[DP-01] DEBT AVALANCHE (mathematically optimal):
Order debts by INTEREST RATE (highest first)
Pay minimums on all
Every extra dollar → highest interest debt
Result: Pay least total interest

[DP-02] DEBT SNOWBALL (psychologically powerful):
Order debts by BALANCE (smallest first)
Pay minimums on all
Every extra dollar → smallest balance
Result: Fastest wins, best for motivation

[DP-03] DEBT SNOWFLAKE (flexible add-ons):
Add any extra money (found money, side income, windfalls) to debt
No fixed extra payment — just send anything extra you find
Combined with Avalanche or Snowball

[DP-04] DEBT CONSOLIDATION CHECKLIST:
□ New interest rate lower than weighted average of current debts?
□ No prepayment penalty on new loan?
□ Total interest paid lower even with longer term?
□ Not using consolidation as excuse to accumulate more debt?
□ Underlying spending behavior changed?

DEBT PAYOFF SCRIPTS:

[DS-01] CREDIT CARD INTEREST RATE NEGOTIATION:
"Hi, I've been a customer for [X years] and always paid on time. My current rate is [X]% and I've received offers from [Competitor] at [Y]%. I'd like to keep my account here — is there anything you can do to match or beat that rate?"

[DS-02] BALANCE TRANSFER NEGOTIATION:
"I'm considering a balance transfer to a 0% card. Before I do that, I wanted to give you the opportunity to offer a reduced rate. What's the best rate you can offer me on my current balance?"

[DS-03] HARDSHIP PROGRAM REQUEST:
"I'm experiencing a temporary financial hardship due to [brief reason]. I want to stay current on my account and am committed to paying. What hardship programs do you have that could temporarily reduce my payment or interest rate?"

[DS-04] COLLECTION ACCOUNT SETTLEMENT SCRIPT:
"I'd like to settle this account. My financial situation is [brief]. I can pay [$X] as a lump sum settlement. In return, I need this removed from my credit report [if applicable, note this must be negotiated]."

[DS-05] STUDENT LOAN INCOME-DRIVEN REPAYMENT:
"I'd like to switch to an income-driven repayment plan. My current income is $[X]. What options am I eligible for and what would my new monthly payment be under each plan?"
